# My company is registered, which documents do I need to open an account?

For regulatory reasons, we need to verify some of your information before the opening of your future Qonto business account:

**1 - Your ID**
===============

You can take a photo of one of one of the following valid ID documents (see [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951040015633)):

* An identity card (front and back)
* A European passport (both pages)
* A Spanish residence permit (front and back)

All information must be clearly visible. We advise you to pay attention to the quality of the photo to activate your account as soon as possible.

Please note that only a **representative or legal representative** of the company can open a Qonto account on behalf of a company. You will need the following documents:

* For **legal entities** and **sole proprietorships**: If your company has been recently incorporated or is in the process of registering with the Commercial Registry, we will need the company's Deeds of incorporation or Form 036.

* During the account opening process, you will have the option to upload your self-employed registration document (Certificate of Census Status, Form 036 or 037, or CIRCE), which you can download directly from the [Tax Agency website](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/en_gb/procedimientoini/G313.shtml). You will also need to provide your registration document from Social Security (which can be downloaded from the [Social Security website](https://portal.seg-social.gob.es/wps/portal/importass/importass/Categorias/Vida+laboral+e+informes/Informes+sobre+tu+situacion+laboral/Informe+de+tu+vida+laboral)). Once we have received and reviewed these documents, we will verify and activate your account.

* For **representatives**: A notarized power of attorney authorizing you to open, manage, and close accounts on behalf of the company.

**2 - The Ultimate Beneficiaries' Owners (UBO) ID**
===================================================

If your company is a partnership or a limited company, in addition to the documents mentioned above (regarding the person opening the Qonto account), you will need to provide valid identification for each beneficial owner of the company.

☝🏼Beneficial owners are natural persons who directly or indirectly own more than 25% of the company's capital, or who have voting rights on decisions affecting the company.

**3 -** **Company registration documents**
==========================================

To verify your company's legal status, please provide a copy of one of the following documents:

* **Beneficial ownership certificate** or **Ultimate beneficial ownership (UBO) declaration**
* **Deeds of incorporation**

These documents should be current and clearly show the company's ownership structure and legal formation details.
