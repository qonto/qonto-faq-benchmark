# Where can I download my bank details?

You can find your bank details with your IBAN and BIC/SWIFT directly from your Qonto app.

Your account details, which include your IBAN and BIC/SWIFT, are available on your Qonto app (both web and mobile).

### **Web app**

In your web application, you have two options:

Option 1: Click on the **"Business account"** section, then **"Transactions"**. Click on the **"Accounts details"** button, then **"Download (PDF)"** to obtain the file in PDF format or **"Copy"** to get it in text format.

Option 2: You can also click on **"Business account"** then **"Accounts"**, and select your main account or one of your sub-accounts if you have any.

☝️ Tips: you can download your account details in a foreign language (🇫🇷 🇬🇧 🇪🇸 🇮🇹 🇩🇪) when you click on the **"Download (PDF)"** button from the web app only.

### **Mobile app**

1. From your mobile application, go to **"Menu",**
2. Navigate to the **"Accounts (IBAN)"** section and select the account of your choice
3. Choose **"Download PDF"** to download your Bank Account Details in PDF format or **"Share IBAN"** to copy the Bank Account Details in text format.

☝️ On the mobile version, you can download your account details only in the language of the country where your company is based.

To obtain a **certified account details (IBAN)**, please contact our customer support via the small chat bubble on your web or mobile application 💬 or by email at [**support@qonto.com**](mailto:support@qonto.com).
