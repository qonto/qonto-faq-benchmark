# What are the different roles available?

Each member's access is defined by a **role**. A role sets the limits of possible actions on a Qonto account.

There are five types of roles:

* The **Account Holder** can access all sections and perform any action: invite or disable access of any members.

* The **Admin** role is granted the same rights as the Account Holder. However, unlike the Account Holder, an Admin is not allowed to request the account closure.

* The **Manager** role (*[for Business and Enterprise plans](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?select-toggle=custom&periodicity=yearly)*) is customizable so that they can only see and do what the Admin or the Owner have decided. They can have access to expense and supplier invoices management, team management, financial monitoring, and bookkeeping. However, they can't set up or manage accounts, change role status, or edit subscriptions.

  ​

  + Their scope of permissions will apply either on a **Team** level or at the **Whole company** level. 👉 You will find the details of their permissions [here](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949964778001).

    Both Basic and Advanced Manager roles have access to the Client Invoices feature.

* The **Employee** role limits access is limited to his own transactions, receipts, and uploaded invoices. They can own a card and make payments with it if one is assigned to them. With Business and Enterprise plans, they are also able to make transfer requests.

* The **Accountant** role (*[from Smart plan](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?select-toggle=solo)*) has been designed to suit the needs of your external accountant, finance advisor, manager, or CPA. With Business and Enterprise plans, they are also able to make transfer requests.

We recommend assigning a role that fits each team member's needs to make the most out of Qonto and achieve maximum collective efficiency.

You can customize approval workflows for transfer and reimbursement requests, allowing you to define which roles can validate a request depending on the amount you set. If you have a Team Business or Enterprise plan, please visit [How to customize workflow approval for transfer and reimbursement requests?](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26572788437137-How-to-customize-workflow-approval-for-transfer-and-reimbursement-requests) to find out more.

For more details, check this article *👉 [How to invite a new team member](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949973149841-How-to-invite-and-manage-team-members-within-my-Qonto-account)*

*💡️ Note: All roles can access both the main account and all sub-accounts. The only difference is in the activities that can be performed within the account. There is currently no way to restrict the access to a specific sub-account.*
