# What documents do I need to open a Qonto account for my cooperative based in Italy?

List of documents required by Qonto to open a business account for cooperatives.

**1 - Personal Identity documents:**
------------------------------------

For Chairman of the Board of Directors and Deputy Chairman of the Board of Directors (if any):

* One valid identity documents. You can find the complete [list of accepted documents here.](https://qonto9015-it.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949991315473)

**2 - Documents related to the company:**

* Visura ordinaria

👉 Only the President or a natural person expressly delegated by him can open a Qonto account in the name of the company (more information [here](https://qonto9015-it.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949961087889)).

👉 Cooperatives - With reference to the updates on the collegiate administration of cooperatives in the 2018 Budget Law (Law 27 December 2017, no. 205, Article 1 paragraph 936), the presence of at least 3 directors is required.
