# Which documents do I need to open an account in germany?

As part of our regulatory review, we require proof of identity from you to open your business account with Qonto. [Learn more here](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/de/articles/23949292113937-Welche-Identit%C3%A4tsnachweise-akzeptiert-Qonto-bei-Ihrer-Registrierung).

​My company is registered
-------------------------

You must be the managing director of your company (as listed in the company's articles of association) to open a business account. If you are opening the account using a power of attorney from the managing director, we require proof of identity from both you and the managing director.

We require the following documents for the following legal forms:

**Sole Proprietor -** Trade License GeWA1 (Issued by your local trade or regulatory office)

**Freelancer -** Official letter from the tax office (Issued by your local tax office)

**(e)GbR -** Trade License GeWA1 & Partners agreement / for eGbR: Current extract from the commercial Register, Transparency register extract & Trade License GeWA1

**GmbH -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**gGmbh -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**UG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**gUG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**AG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**e.K./e.Kfm/e.Kfr -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section A) & Trade License GeWA1

**AG & Co KG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B), commercial register extract of AG & Transparency register extract

**GmbH & Co. KG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B), commercial register extract of GmbH & Transparency register extract

**AG & Co oHG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B), commercial register extract of AG and OHG & Transparency register extract

**UG & Co. KG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B), commercial register extract of UG & Transparency register extract

**oHG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**GmbH & Co. oHG -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B), commercial register extract of GmbH and oHG & Transparency register extract

**Limited Partnership -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section A) & Transparency register extract

**KGaA -** Current extract from the commercial register (Section B) & Transparency register extract

**PartG -** Current extract from the commercial register & Transparency register extract

My company is in formation
--------------------------

During registration, the notarized formation documents must be uploaded. The documents should be stamped and signed or sealed and signed:

* Registration in the commercial register
* Certificate of incorporation
* List of shareholders

**What documents do we need if one of your shareholders is a legal entity?**

If a legal entity appears as a shareholder, certain documents are required to ensure legal integrity and transparency. Please provide us with the following documents:

1. **Certificate of incorporation of the legal entity:** This document confirms the lawful formation and existence of the legal entity. It should contain all relevant details about the formation and structure of the company.
2. **Commercial register extract:** An official extract from the commercial register containing information about the registration of the legal entity and its authorized representatives and managing directors.
3. **List of shareholders:** A list of all shareholders of the legal entity disclosing their shares and interests in the company.
4. **Transparency register extract (if applicable):** If it is a German company, a transparency register extract is required. This document contains information about the beneficial owners and the actual owners of the legal entity according to legal requirements.
5. **Note for legal entities in formation:** If the legal entity is still in the formation process and therefore does not possess a commercial register extract, we alternatively accept the registration in the commercial register. However, this is only possible if the legal entity maintains an account with Qonto.

   💡 ***This only applies to legal entities that have a seat in Germany***

Providing these documents allows us to fulfill the necessary legal due diligence and ensure a smooth business relationship.

Currently, the following legal forms are offered for your foundation:

* GmbH (Limited Liability Company) in formation
* UG (Entrepreneurial Company) in formation
* gGmbH in formation
* gUG in formation
