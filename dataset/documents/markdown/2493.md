# What is OVH integration?

We know managing invoices should not slow you down. That is why we built our Supplier Invoices integrations in-house – to give you back precious time. No more logging into multiple supplier portals or manually uploading invoices. Our OVH integration connects directly to your supplier platform, automatically retrieving your invoices and streamlining your workflow. It is part of our mission to create the freedom for your business to succeed, without the paperwork getting in the way.

**How does the OVH integration work?**
--------------------------------------

It is simple. Install the integration directly from our marketplace in just a few clicks – no technical setup required.

Once connected, your OVH invoices flow automatically into your Qonto account. No manual downloads, no uploads, no hassle. Just seamless invoice management that works in the background while you focus on growing your business.

**How we are solving invoice management ?**
-------------------------------------------

We are building smart integrations for each supplier platform that put you in control:

**Connect effortlessly** – Link your supplier account and choose which Qonto account receives your invoices.

**Catch up instantly** – Select a start date up to 90 days back to import your recent invoices.

**Stay up-to-date automatically** – New invoices sync daily and match to your transactions using smart OCR technology. When we can not find an automatic match, we will simply ask you to confirm manually.

It is invoice management that works around your schedule, not the other way around.

**What do I need to set up the OVH integration?**
-------------------------------------------------

Before installing the integration, make sure you have:

* An active OVHCloud account with billing enabled and at least Business or Enterprise Support Level on OVH account
* Access to your two-factor authentication (2FA) as it may be required during setup

**How does the setup work?**

The installation is simple and guided:

**Step 1: Connect your account** You will authenticate with your OVHCloud credentials to securely link your accounts.

**Step 2: Choose your sync preferences** Select how far back you would like us to import your invoices (up to 90 days). If you skip this step, we will automatically fetch the last 90 days of invoices to get you started.

That's it – your OVH invoices will start flowing into Qonto automatically.
