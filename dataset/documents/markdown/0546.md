# Can I have a LCR letter?

*This article is related to companies registered in France 🇫🇷*

The "*Lettre de Change Relevé*" (or LCR) is a means of payment that binds a customer to his supplier.

To date, Qonto does not yet allow you to issue this document physically or virtually.

But know that there are services that allow you to set up direct debits very simply:

* [Get paid via direct debits](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/25012148623249-How-to-collect-payments-by-direct-debit-via-EBICS)
* [Pay your invoices via direct debits](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947694535569-How-do-SEPA-Direct-Debits-work)
