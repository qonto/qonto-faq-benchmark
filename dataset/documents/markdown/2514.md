# What exactly is Cash Flow Management in Qonto and how can it help my business?

Cash Flow Management is dashboard and series of tools that give you complete visibility over your finances 24/7. It helps you track your real-time cash balance, analyze spending patterns, forecast upcoming cash flow, and make informed business decisions without needing external financial expertise.

These tools automatically categorize your transactions and provide actionable insights to help you keep your business finances on track.

Access your Cash Flow Management features through either the web application by clicking **Cash Flow Management** in the left navigation and selecting **Forecast**, or via the mobile app by tapping **Menu** at the bottom right of your screen and selecting **Forecast**.

Both options give you full access to your cash flow monitoring tools and insights.

### **How can I access these features?**

Access depends on your subscription level :

* Dashboard access: Available to all users for basic data visualization of historic transactions
* Home screen features: Core functionalities accessible from the main interface
* Insights section: Advanced analytics and suggestions for higher-tier subscriptions
* Full cash flow product: Complete suite of tools for comprehensive financial tracking and planning—including forecasts and performance analysis

### **What Cash Flow Management features are included in each plan?**

Each plan offers different levels of functionality:

**Basic Plan**:

* Historical cash flow analysis with ready-to-use charts
* Auto-categorization with Qonto default categories

**Smart Plan** and above (**Smart, Premium, Essential, Business, Enterprise**):

* Everything in Basic, plus:
* Customizable category management
* Real-time VAT calculation and updates
* Interactive cash flow table with detailed transaction views
* Ability to track spending patterns
* Full customization of cash flow categories

**Cash Flow Management Add On**

* Everything on the Smart Plan and above plus:
* Cash flow forecasting
* Flash forecast using Qonto Intelligence or CSV upload to import your Excel forecast into Qonto
* Access to Cash compass where you can track upcoming client invoices, subscriptions, SDD, and supplier payments and take action to optimize your cash flow from one dashboard.

All plans include historical analysis and automatic categorization, but for advanced features like VAT calculation, interactive tables, and custom categories, you will need Smart plan or higher. The Basic plan provides essential cash flow monitoring tools, while higher tiers offer more sophisticated analysis and customization options.

When you activate Cash Flow Management, you get:

* A 30-day free trial with access to all features
* No charges during the trial period
* Email notifications before trial expiration
* Automatic transition to paid subscription after trial

After your trial period:

* Your subscription will automatically convert to a paid plan if you don't cancel
* You will receive email reminders before any charges

### **What specific information can I see in my Cash Flow dashboard?**

The Cash Flow Management Add On includes exclusive features not available in other modules (Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, or Expense & Spend Management):

* Complete view of past and future cash flows
* Up to 5 unique cash flow forecast scenarios
* Projected financial positions
* Visibility on upcoming transactions
* Dashboard showing future client invoices, subscriptions, and supplier payments

### **How does the automatic transaction categorization work?**

* Automatically categorizes transactions into predefined groups (Travel, Taxes, etc.)
* Updates categories for past, present, and future payments
* Allows you to customize categories and tags for your specific business needs
* Handles about 60% of transactions automatically through recurring payment detection

### **What kind of alerts and notifications can I receive?**

* Real-time notifications for significant spending changes
* Alerts when cash balance falls below certain thresholds
* Notifications for unexpected cash flow patterns
* Alerts for notable spending trends in specific categories
* Daily or periodic balance updates (customizable)
