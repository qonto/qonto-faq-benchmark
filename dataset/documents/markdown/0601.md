# How does Regate by Qonto automate your bookkeeping?

You’re a certified public accountant or a bookkeeper? Regate by Qonto is a tool designed for you to manage each customer up to three times faster.

### What makes it unique?

Regate by Qonto enhances collaboration by automatically retrieving financial documents and transactions from your Qonto customer. This ensures that you always have access to up-to-date data: which you can automatically transform into accounting records. The tool is integrated with your preferred production tool: no migration needed!

### Can I try it for free with one of my customers?

Yes, experience the streamlined process by trying Regate by Qonto for free with one of the organizations you have access to via your accountant access.

### How does it work?

From the web app, please click [here](https://web.regate.io/qonto/oauth) and follow the steps below:

1. Input and verify your email address.
2. Enter your first, last name and your phone number.
3. Choose a password.
4. Select the date you want to retrieve financial documents & transactions from.
5. Enjoy the full value of Regate by Qonto.
