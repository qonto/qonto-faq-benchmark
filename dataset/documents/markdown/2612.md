# How can I reimburse a team member for an expense paid with their own money?

*The **Expense reimbursement** feature is available [on Smart, Premium, Essential, Business and Enterprise plans](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?select-toggle=team).*

**Expense reimbursement** allows all team members to submit reimbursements for any company expenses paid by personal funds. Reimbursement requests can be approved by Admins and Managers with the transfer permissions on.

How do I get informed an Expense **reimbursement** request has been submitted?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Owners, Admins, and Advanced Managers roles with the transfer permissions activated receive an email and mobile notification as soon as a request has been submitted.

Learn more about [how to customize Qonto’s notifications](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950985386641).

**How can I approve and decline an Expense reimbursement request?**
-------------------------------------------------------------------

**On desktop 💻**

1. Select the **Team expenses** section on the left
2. Click on **Reimbursement**
3. On the **To approve** tab, select an expense
4. Select an account to debit for this reimbursement and **Approve** the request
5. Or **Decline** it and write a rejection reason

**On Mobile📱**

1. Open the Qonto App on your device
2. Click on **Requests**, then select the Expense report request
3. Click on **Pay by transfer** to approve the request or **Decline** and write a rejection reason

Once the **Expense reimbursement** request has been approved, the requestor will receive an instant reimbursement.

All reimbursement requests can be part of a customizable approval workflow. Please visit [How to customize workflow approval for transfer and reimbursement requests?](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26624250395921/) to find out more about multi-layer approval workflows to maintain control over large amounts to reimburse or pay with configurable approvals.

**As an Owner, Admin, and Advanced Manager with the transfer permissions activated, how do I get reimbursed for an expense I paid with my own money?**
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To get reimbursed for a professional expense, Owners, Admins, or Advanced Managers with transfer permissions can follow the same procedure as above by approving their own submissions. They will then receive the reimbursement via an instant transfer.

To get reimbursed faster, the Owner, Admin, or Advanced Manager with the transfer permissions can send an [instant transfer](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950961998481) by activating on toggle **Instant transfer**.
