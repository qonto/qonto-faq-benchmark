# Can I get a checkbook or a check?

Can I make check payments ?

As of today, **we do not offer neither checkbooks, or checks**.

Therefore, it is not possible to make check payments with your account.

Indeed, Qonto is an online payment institution, which offers exclusively dematerialized services.

However, we allow **cashing** checks on an exceptional basis if your customers have no other means of payment.

👉 [How much is charged for cashing a check?](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951054562705)

💡 We advise you to **use transfer payments**, which offer more advantages, both in efficiency and security. [Learn more about transfers](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/categories/23671269624209-Managing-my-transfers-direct-debits-and-checks).
