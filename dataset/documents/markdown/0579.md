# How to deposit a check?

🇫🇷 *Check cashing is available exclusively in euros for the French market*

### How to deposit a check **on your desktop application**?

1. Access your Qonto account
2. Click on the **Business Account** section, then on **Check deposit**
3. You will need to **activate the FNCI mandate** in order to complete the deposit.
   ​[You can click here to read our article dedicated to this subject](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947595117073).
4. Fill in the check information
5. If the FNCI result of your check is:

   * **Positive**: you can confirm your deposit and follow step 6.
   * **Irregular check**: we recommend that you abandon the deposit. If you confirm it and we receive your cheque, it will be rejected.
6. Send us your check by mail **within 2 weeks** to the address that appears on your screen. If we do not receive the check in time, we won’t be able to register the transaction.

If you wish to be guided on your desktop app, please click here: [https://app.qonto.com?tdm=deposit-check](https://app.qonto.com/?tdm=deposit-check)

### How to deposit a check **on your mobile application**?

1. Open the check section in the **More** tab
2. Click on **Deposit a check** and follow the instructions
3. You will need to **activate the FNCI mandate** in order to complete the deposit.
   ​[You can click here to read our article dedicated to this subject](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947595117073).
4. Fill in the check information
5. If the FNCI result of your check is:

   * **Positive**: you can confirm your deposit and follow step 6.
   * **Irregular check**: we recommend that you abandon the deposit. If you confirm it and we receive your cheque, it will be rejected.
6. Send us your check by mail **within 2 weeks** to the address that appears on your screen. If we do not receive the check in time, we won’t be able to register the transaction.

*Note: You need to endorse (*sign on the back) *a check to deposit it.*

Accepted checks:

* Bank checks
* French checks
* Checks in euros

### **What are the cheque deposit limits?**

The amount collected by check cannot exceed €30,000 per 30 running days, within the limit of €15,000 per single check. Checks must be made out to the legal company name (therefore, make sure it matches the name on your Qonto account).

### **How can I cancel my check deposit?**

It is not possible to cancel the deposit of your check from your customer space. You will need to contact customer support directly to do so.
