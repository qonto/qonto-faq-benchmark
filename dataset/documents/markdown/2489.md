# Do you think you are the victim of a fraud via a phone call (phishing)?

If someone pretending to be a Qonto agent contacts you by phone to report suspected fraud on your account and asks you to urgently carry out or validate operations (card transactions, transfers), **hang up immediately.**

If a Qonto agent contacts you by phone to report suspected fraud on your account and asks you to urgently carry out or validate operations (card transactions, transfers), **hang up immediately.**

Do not share any personal or confidential information by phone, text message or via an email received, these are fake (IBAN - account number, card numbers, password, email address, etc.).

Indeed, fraudsters use this technique to embezzle funds.

**Qonto will never contact you to ask you to validate card transactions or make transfers.**
