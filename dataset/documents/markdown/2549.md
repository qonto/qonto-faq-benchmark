# How do I edit or cancel a scheduled international transfer?

After validating a transfer, under certain circumstances you still have the possibility to edit or cancel it directly from the mobile and the web app.

**Where can I view my scheduled international transfers?**
----------------------------------------------------------

An overview of your transfers is available in the **Business account** menu's **Transfers** section. Your scheduled yet to be processed transfers will appear in the **Processing** tab while the already completed and failed transfers will appear in the **Completed** tab.

**💡 Good to know:** only completed transfers appear in the **Transactions** view. If a scheduled transfer is created through the **Supplier Invoices** section, the invoice related to this transfer will also appear in the **Scheduled Payments** tab of the section.

**How do I edit or cancel them?**
---------------------------------

### **💻 On your web app**

1. Click on **Business account** and go to your transfers overview by clicking on **Transfers**.
2. Go to the **Processing** tab and select the transfer that you would like to edit or cancel.

* To edit the transfer, click on **Edit**.
  + Make the necessary adjustments and then click on **Confirm**.
  + Complete the two-factor authentication to validate the change.
* To cancel the transfer, click on **Cancel**.
  + Confirm your action by clicking on **Confirm** in the confirmation prompt.

### **📱On your mobile app**

1. Go to your transfers overview by tapping on **Transfers**.
2. Tap on the transfer that you would like to edit or cancel.

* To edit the transfer, tap on **Edit transfer**.
  + Make the necessary adjustments and then tap on **Confirm**.
  + Complete the two-factor authentication to validate the change.
* To cancel a transfer, tap on **Cancel transfer**.
  + Confirm your action by tapping on **Confirm** in the confirmation prompt.

**Are there any editing limitations?**
--------------------------------------

There are a couple of limitations to which transfer details can be edited once a scheduled or recurring transfer has been set up.

* The amount, the account to be debited and the beneficiary **cannot be edited anymore**. If you want to edit these details, you have to cancel the transfer and set up a new one.
* The frequency and the end date of a recurring transfer are editable in both the mobile and the web app. **The execution starting date can only be changed in the mobile app.**
