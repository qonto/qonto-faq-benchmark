# How to obtain a certificate or attestation via Qonto?

Do you need an official document, such as proof of payment or a Qonto business Mastercard ownership certificate, for your professional or administrative procedures? Qonto offers a wide range of certificates to meet your needs. Here’s everything you need to know about requesting them based on your role and the options available.

Where can I download my certified account details?
--------------------------------------------------

Your certified account details, which include your IBAN and BIC/SWIFT, are available in your Qonto app (both web and mobile).

### From your web app:

In your web application, you have two options:

1. Go to "**Business account**"
2. Then you have two options:
   - go to "**Transactions**" **>** "**Account details**"
   - OR go to "**Accounts**" **>** select your main account
3. Select the language in "Get your account certificate" and click on "**Download PDF**" to download the file in PDF format.

☝️ Tips: You can download your certified account details either in English or the language of the country where your company is based.



### From your mobile app:

1. Go to "**Menu**",
2. Navigate to the "**Accounts (IBAN)**" section and select the account of your choice,
3. Then "**Account certificate**"
4. Select "**Download PDF**" to download your certificate in PDF format.

What certificates can I request to the Qonto Support?
-----------------------------------------------------

You can request one of the certificates below by contacting Qonto support directly through the chat bubble in your app (both on mobile and website).

### 1. Cards & Checks

* Non-coverage certificate by Qonto
* Qonto business Mastercard ownership certificate
* Qonto card payment certificate

### 2. Account Management

* Proof of transfer (SATD - France)
* Proof of transfer (SA - France)
* Know Your Customer (KYC) certificate
* Account closure confirmation
* Loan ineligibility certificate

### 3. Transfers & Direct Debits

* Proof of payment
* Certificate of funds provenance
* Proof of non-receipt of funds
* Certificate of receipt of funds

Where can I download a proof of transfer?
-----------------------------------------

1. Log in to your Qonto account.
2. Go to the **Transfers** tab.
3. Click on the relevant transaction.
4. Download the proof of transfer.

Who can request a certificate?
------------------------------

* **Account Holder (Owner) and Admin**

You can request a certificate and will receive it directly. The account holder will automatically be copied in the email upon sending.

* **Manager, Employee, or Accountant**

These roles can request a certificate, but **the document will not be sent directly to them.** Instead, the certificate will be sent to the account holder, who can then share it with them.
