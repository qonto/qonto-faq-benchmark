# Who can open a Qonto account on behalf of my company?

You can open an account if:

1. You are an individual legal representative of the company:

* + **Sole Administrator**
  + **Joint and Several Administrator**
  + **President or CEO**
  + **Joint Administrator**

👉🏻 A legal representative is a person who has the authority to act on behalf of the company in legal and business matters.
