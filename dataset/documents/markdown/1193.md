# How do I register my existing business with Qonto?

Want to set up a Qonto account for your existing business? **It's simple and time-saving in just a few steps.**

**You will need the following documents and information**

* 📄 Your company data or commercial register number
* 👥 Your valid accepted [identity document](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/de/articles/23949292113937-Welche-Identit%C3%A4tsnachweise-akzeptiert-Qonto-bei-Ihrer-Registrierung)
* The data of your company's beneficial owners
* If you are not a legal representative, a power of attorney
* A smartphone, tablet, or a computer with a camera & microphone. 💡 The Google Chrome browser is 100% compatible with Qonto.

Let's get started **⬇️**
------------------------

**First, go to the [Qonto.de](http://qonto.de/) website and click on the "Open an account" button at the top right of your screen.**



Your Company Data
-----------------

Inform us that your company is based in **Germany** so your case can be handled by the appropriate team.

**Select the legal form** of your company.

**Enter the email address** that will serve as your username when logging into your Qonto interface.

**Check your mailbox** to retrieve the confirmation code and then enter it in the respective field. ❗️The confirmation code is only valid for 30 minutes.

**Search for your company** by entering the company name or commercial register number and clicking "Search". You will then be asked to confirm your company's details.

* If you can't find your company, select the option "Add information manually" and enter your company details.

Your Personal Data
------------------

**Enter your personal data and indicate whether you are a legal representative.**

* If you are the legal representative of the company, select your name and confirm your details.
* ❗️ If you are not the legal representative of the company, you must upload a **power of attorney** from the legal representative to you, as well as their **identity document**.

Beneficial Owners
-----------------

**Provide all beneficial owners of the company.**

* ❗️It is crucial that you provide all other beneficial owners of your company. A beneficial owner is a natural person who:

  + owns more than 25% of the company's capital or voting rights
  + exercises control over the company in another form

Subscription & Agreement
------------------------

Choose the **subscription and add-on** that best **meets your needs** and accept the **terms and conditions**.

Order Qonto Card
----------------

Order the [**Qonto card of your choice**](https://qonto.com/de/payment-methods/card) and confirm the **delivery address**.

Confirm Your Identity
---------------------

**Important!**To fully utilize your account, a **video identification or eID with our partner IDnow is required.**

* have your smartphone, laptop, or tablet with a good stable internet connection, camera, and microphone ready
* have your identity document (ID, passport, German residence permit) ready
* the environment should be quiet and well-lit
* **Start the video call with IDnow to fully utilize your account as soon as possible!**



Done! Once you receive your IBAN, you can immediately fund your Qonto account [via SEPA real-time, standard transfer](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/de/articles/23949246923409-Wie-kann-ich-eine-%C3%9Cberweisung-auf-mein-Qonto-Gesch%C3%A4ftskonto-vornehmen) or [by card deposit.](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/de/articles/23949267077265-Wie-kann-ich-mein-Qonto-Konto-mit-einer-Karte-aufladen)

Your account is typically **validated by our team within a maximum of 24 hours** and is now fully operational!
