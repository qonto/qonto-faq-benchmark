# Can my Qonto account be overdrawn?

Your account **cannot be overdrawn.** That's why we always check your balance before authorizing any payment.

Please make sure that your balance enables you to honour your commitments.

**The low-balance status makes it impossible for you to process any new payment** - including card payments, transfers, and direct debits - until you [top up your account](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949246923409).

If the balance of your account is low, you'll receive an alert. This occurs when your current balance does not allow you to honor transfers, subscriptions, and direct debits **scheduled in the next 14 days**.

**👉Please note:** If your company is eligible, you are welcome to use our **Pay Later** offer ([How to use Qonto’s Pay later feature?](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/24275960254609-How-to-use-Qonto-s-Pay-later-feature)). You can also find our partner's financing offers in the **Financing** section of your Qonto app.
