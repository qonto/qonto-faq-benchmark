# What is the Insurance section and how does it work?

The **Insurance** section in Qonto allows you to explore and purchase a wide range of insurance products tailored to your business needs. On this platform, you will find offers from two insurance partners: **Stello 🇫🇷** and **SureIn 🇩🇪**.

There you will find an insurance marketplace with a **simple, transparent, and easily accessible solution** to obtain insurance with our partners. **Keep track of your insurance at any time with an overview** of status, duration, and premium payment.

Who can use this feature?
-------------------------

As a Qonto customer, you can access the insurance section in France and Germany regardless of your pricing plan if your main business activity falls into one of the following areas:

* Digital
* Construction
* Hospitality/Retail
* Transport/Taxi
* Regulated/licensed professions

👉Note: The use of the insurance section is available via the web app and only for the roles of account holder and admin.

What types of insurance products are available in the insurance section?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

We offer a variety of insurance products tailored to different industries.

* In **Germany**, we offer you **business liability insurance, financial loss liability insurance, cyber insurance, legal business insurance, business electronics insurance and business contents insurance.**
* In France, 12 insurance products are available, including professional liability insurance, legal protection, and insurance for computer accessories.

The displayed products are filtered based on your company's activity to ensure relevance.

Which insurance providers do we work with?
------------------------------------------

We collaborate with well-known insurance platforms such as **Stello** and **SureIn**. Each insurance product can be offered by different providers, including AIG, Allcura, Allianz, AIG, AndSafe, Axa, Beazley, ERGO, Gothaer, HDI, Hiscox, Markel, SIGNAL IDUNA, VHV, Zurich, and others, with different rates available for each provider.

How do I apply for an insurance product?
----------------------------------------

To apply for insurance through Qonto, visit the **Insurance** section in your web app. There you will find all the insurance products relevant to your company.

By clicking on a product, you will receive more information and have the option to click on **Request Quote**. You will then be redirected to the insurer's platform (e.g., SureIn) to fill out your application or be contacted by a representative, depending on the product you have chosen.

Why should I apply through Qonto and not directly on the partner's website?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Applying through Qonto provides a smooth, integrated experience that allows you to **manage all your insurance contracts directly from your Qonto account.** Additionally, we ensure that the application process meets our **quality standards, guaranteeing an optimal user experience.**
