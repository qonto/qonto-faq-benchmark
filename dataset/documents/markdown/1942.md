# What are the different Qonto cards ?

*Qonto has received an order from Bank of Italy to enhance its anti-money laundering measures in its Italian branch. For this reason the subscription of new debit cards is temporarily suspended. At the moment you can only renew expiring or lost cards.*

If you already have a Qonto card 👇

**One Card**
------------

Free according to your subscription or 5€/month (excl. tax)

* Mastercard Business Debit
* Pay up to €20,000 per calendar month
* Withdraw up to €1,000 per calendar month
* Unlimited and free payment in Europe
* 2€ (excl. VAT) / withdrawal
* 2% foreign exchange commission on non-euro payments (and no fixed fees)
* Produced from recycled ocean plastic

**Plus Card**
-------------

€8 / month (excl. tax)

* Mastercard World Debit
* Color customization: silver, black or purple
* Choose between embossed or printed cards
* Pay up to €40,000 per calendar month
* Withdraw up to €2,000 per calendar month
* Unlimited and free payment in Europe
* 5 free withdrawals then 2€ (excl. VAT) / withdrawal
* 1% foreign exchange commission on non-euro payments (and no fixed fees)
* Benefit from advanced card insurance. More info [here](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/6845080-what-is-my-card-coverage-insurance)
* Produced from recycled ocean plastic

**X Card**
----------

€20 / month (excl. tax)

* Mastercard World Debit
* Color customization: black, silver or gold
* Benefit from a 0% foreign exchange commission on non-euro payments (and no fixed fees)
* Pay up to

  + €200,000 per calendar month **if you are enrolled SCA** (Strong Customer Authentication)
  + €60 000 per month **if you haven’t activate your SCA**. We strongly advise you to activate this strong authentification, following these steps : **[How to pair my phone with my Qonto account?](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949957227025)**
* Withdraw up to €3,000 for free, per calendar month

  (💡 If you are registered to a german account, you can benefit from higher withdrawal limits. Please klick [here](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/6432685-additional-information-about-x-card-withdrawal-limits) for more information.)
* Take full advantage of a concierge service available 7 days a week
* Access airport lounges
* Benefit from Qonto’s most exclusive card insurance. More info [here](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949963006609-How-does-my-card-insurance-work)

Qonto virtual cards
===================

If you already have a virtual card you can make online payments up to €20,000 per calendar month.

---

Qonto Online advertising cards
==============================

Online advertising card are **virtual cards** specifically designed to help you manage your marketing campaigns across top ads platforms and merchants like Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and other.

---

Qonto Instant cards
===================

[Instant cards](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/5440624-what-is-flash-card-and-how-to-use-it) are temporary virtual cards. They are ideal for teams’ one-off expenses. No need to share a card or pay out-of-pocket!

---

Qonto fuel cards
================

Qonto does not offer a fuel card per se. However, if you already have a physical card, you can easily restrict its use solely to gas stations from the specific spending category section. You can also define the days it can be used and set a payment limit according to your needs. These options are available only for the Essential, Business, and Enterprise plans.
