# What is IDnow VideoIdent?

IDnow VideoIdent is a secure and legally recognized video-based identity verification process. A trained specialist verifies your personal data and documents during a live video call to reliably confirm your identity.

### In which languages is the video identification available?

Video identification with IDnow is currently available in the following languages:

* German
* English
* French
* Spanish

### Do I have to complete the video identification in one of these languages?

Yes. The entire identification process must be conducted fully in one of the supported languages.

### Am I allowed to use an interpreter during the video identification?

**🚨 No, interpreters are not permitted during IDnow VideoIdent.**

The participant must be able to communicate independently in one of the supported languages. This is a legal requirement for successful identity verification.

### How does the video identification process work technically?

1. You will receive a link or be redirected in the app to start the VideoIdent process.
2. During a live video call, you will show your official ID (identity card, passport, etc.) to an IDnow agent.
3. The agent verifies your documents and may ask questions to confirm your identity.
4. The process typically takes only a few minutes.
5. After successful verification, you receive confirmation, and the process is complete.

### Which documents are accepted?

Valid official ID documents such as identity cards and passports are accepted. Find out more here: [What kind of identity documents are accepted by Qonto in Germany?](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949292113937-What-kind-of-identity-documents-are-accepted-by-Qonto-in-germany)

### What if I don’t speak any of the supported languages?

If you do not speak one of the supported languages, the identification cannot be completed. Please contact our customer support for possible alternative solutions.

### Is the VideoIdent process secure?

Yes. IDnow complies with the highest security and data protection standards. All data is transmitted encrypted and used solely for identity verification purposes.

### Where can I get help if I encounter problems with the video identification?

If you have any technical difficulties or questions, please contact our customer support team. We are happy to assist you at every step.
