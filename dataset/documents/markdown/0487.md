# What is the free Qonto Invoicing account?

Qonto Invoicing is a free account designed specifically for **freelancers**, **micro-businesses**, or clients whose accountants use **Regate by Qonto**.

This offer gives you access to a financial management platform.

However, it is not a traditional Qonto business account because:

* It does not include an IBAN
* It does not allow banking transactions (transfers, card payments, etc.)

This platform enables you to connect external bank accounts via Qonto’s bank aggregation feature, allowing you to benefit from Qonto’s accounting management tools.

As part of the rollout of electronic invoicing in France, it also provides a regulation-compliant solution, helping you optimize the management of your receipts and invoices.

Who can access Qonto Invoicing?
-------------------------------

This offer is open to all businesses based in France with an active SIRET/SIREN number.

It is also available to Regate by Qonto clients, upon invitation from their accountant.

Platforms available: Web and mobile app

How to create a Qonto Invoicing account?
----------------------------------------

**From Regate by Qonto:**
You will be redirected to Qonto to create your Qonto Invoicing account. Some information will be automatically retrieved to streamline the account creation process.

**From Qonto:**
You can create an account via this [link](https://welcome.qonto.com/qonto-invoicing/signup/credentials/validate-email)

* Enter your email address
* Create your password
* Enter your phone number
* Provide your full name and SIRET number

Your Qonto Invoicing account is now created!

What features are included?
---------------------------

Qonto Invoicing accounts allow clients to benefit from the following management tools:

### 1. Bank account aggregation

* [Connection of external accounts](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/24231375285777) for centralized monitoring
* Visibility over all transactions
* [Attachment of supplier invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/26715651026193) to external transactions
* Tracking of invoice payments from external bank accounts

### 2. Supplier invoice management

* [List of suppliers](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/29128103953169)
* [Storage and management of supplier invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/23947625621777)

### 3. Customer invoice management

* [Invoicing](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/23947647770897) and payment tracking
* [Issuing](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/23947623005201) and [managing quotes](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/fr/articles/23947624769553)
* List of products and services
* List of clients
* Accountant access (available only for Regate by Qonto clients)

All these features are similar to those available in traditional Qonto accounts, except for banking services (IBAN, card, transfers, etc.).

How do I add a bank account to my Qonto Invoicing account?
----------------------------------------------------------

If you wish to add a bank account to your Qonto Invoicing account, you’ll need to choose a plan from our range of paid offers.

You can then upgrade your Qonto plan via the mobile app or desktop, with a choice of monthly or annual billing. You’ll get one free month to try out the new plan.

🖥️ **From your computer**: Go to *Settings* (bottom left) > *Plan & invoices* > *Plan details*

📲 **From your mobile app**: Go to *Menu* > "⚙️" > *Plan*

Then:

* Select **Change plan**
* Compare our offers by browsing between our plans for freelancers and team plans for SMEs and startups, depending on your needs
* Select the desired plan and confirm by clicking **Choose**

If you'd like to switch back to a Qonto Invoicing account, feel free to contact our support team directly via chat or email.
