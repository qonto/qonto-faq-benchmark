# Can I open a Qonto account to make a capital deposit?

For legal reasons, in Spain the capital deposit during company formation must be made with a credit institution. Qonto, being a **payment institution** registered with the Bank of Spain, cannot issue capital deposit certificates that are legally valid before a notary.
