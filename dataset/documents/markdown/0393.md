# How does the Supplier invoices section work?

Automate the processing of your receipts and streamline the payment of your suppliers

With the **Supplier Invoices** section, you can manage your supplier invoices conveniently from a single tab, the **Inbox**:

1. Collect your invoices
2. Attach your receipts and paid invoices to the transactions [(Learn more)](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947627750929).
3. Easily prioritize overdue invoices, review details, and make payments in just a few clicks.

Once an invoice is matched or its payment is created, Qonto removes it from your Inbox, leaving only the invoices that require your attention!

This feature is accessible on all plans, via mobile and web apps, fort he following roles:

* Owner and Admins roles
* Manager roles with "Whole Company scope" and the "Upload and set up supplier invoices" permission. They can also make direct payments to suppliers (within transfer limits) if they have the "Make and approve transfers" permission.
* Accountants roles with view-only mode.

💡 Note: The **Supplier Invoices** section is not for processing [customer invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947647770897).

You can also set up multi-level approval workflows to maintain control over large reimbursement or payment amounts. These approval workflows are available through the **Accounts Payable** and **Expense & Spend Management** [add-ons](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28479048690705). Learn more about approval workflows [here](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26570070089745).

**How does it work?**
---------------------

To access to the Supplier invoices section:

1. Go to the **Invoices** section :

   1. From the web app, select the **Invoices** icon on the navigation menu
   2. From the mobile app, click **Plus** in the lower right corner
2. Select the **Supplier invoice** section

On the top of your screen, you can get insights on the total amount of:

* Invoices in your inbox
* Invoices in pending
* Invoices for which there is a scheduled payment
* Invoices paid in the last 30 days.

**Collect invoices:**
---------------------

Collect all your invoices, either paid or unpaid, in your **Inbox**:

* Using the **Upload invoices** button of your Supplier Invoices section, or directly **dragging and dropping** your documents at the center of the Imported tab, in the web app.
* From your email box, using [your forwarding email address](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947591709457).
* From [Google Drive](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947658434705) or [Dropbox](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947628575505).
* By synchronising the invoice in transaction attachments

💡 Note: Invoices and receipts added [manually](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947723392273) to transactions from your **History** will not appear in your Supplier invoices section.

**Pay or match invoices:**
--------------------------

1. **Already paid invoices:**

   * Qonto automatically matches receipts and paid invoices to the right transaction and moves the invoice from the **All invoices**to the **Completed** tab of the Supplier Invoices section.
   * If we could not match the invoice to the relevant transaction, go to the supplier invoices section and click on **"...”**  next to the invoice you want to edit, then on **Mark as paid** to manually attach the invoice to the relevant transaction.
   * You can also decide not to match the invoice with a transaction, if you don't wish to associate the invoice with any.
     
   * 💡From the Transactions section you can also search for invoices using the Find on Qonto option.
2. **Unpaid invoices:**

* For imported invoices in the Supplier invoices section, click on the invoice to:

  + Edit the payment details.
  + Pay either with a classic [SEPA](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947676520465) transfer, an i[nternational transfer](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26049704105361), a scheduled SEPA transfer, an [instant transfer](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947629441681). Eligible customers can also take advantage of [Pay Later](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/24475495156881) options to finance their invoice payment.
  + For multiple unpaid invoices, you can select several invoices and process them simultaneously with bulk payment functionality. This allows you to pay several suppliers at once, saving time on repetitive transfers.
  + Once the transfer is created, Qonto moves the invoice to **Completed** in the case of an instant transfer or in the **Scheduled payments** tab for the other type of transfers. For the latter, the invoice will be moved to **Completed** once the transfer is executed.
  + Qonto automatically attaches the invoice to the corresponding transaction in your **Business Account > Transactions** section.
  + You will also be able to download your [proof of transfer](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947726035473) once it has been executed.

Your Supplier invoices area supports bulk invoice payments, allowing you to pay multiple invoices simultaneously. You cannot make [bulk transfers](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947719198225) outside of the supplier invoices section, pay by card, or set up [SEPA direct debits](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947694535569).

Check out 👉 [this article](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947610848017) to learn how Managers can use the Supplier invoices feature.

Process multiple invoices with bulk payments
--------------------------------------------

1. In your Supplier invoices section, select multiple unpaid invoices by checking the boxes next to each invoice
2. Click the on the Pay button
3. Review all payment details in a consolidated view -Qonto automatically validates supplier information, IBANs, and amounts when possible
4. Choose your payment method (SEPA transfer, instant transfer, or scheduled transfer

      5. Confirm and execute all payments in one action

**Match credit notes to an invoice**
------------------------------------

Qonto automatically matches the credit note to the correct transaction and moves the document from the **All Invoices** tab to the **Completed** tab in the **Supplier Invoices** section if we are able to detect the correct transaction ourselves.

If the credit note has not been automatically matched to the relevant transaction, go to the **Supplier Invoices** section, click on "**...**" next to the line you want to edit, and then select **Match Transaction** to manually attach the credit note to the relevant transaction.

You can also choose not to match the credit note with a transaction if you don't wish to associate the document with any.

**Cancel or reschedule payment:**
---------------------------------

To cancel a scheduled transfer through the **Supplier Invoices** section:

1. Check the **[transfer status](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947687064081)** to ensure it hasn't been executed yet. (Learn more about transfer execution dates **[here](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947725223825))**.
2. On the Web app, go to the **Business account** section on the navigation menu, then navigate to **Transactions** or **Transfers**. From the mobile app, go to the **History** or **Transfers** section. Select the transfer you want, and click **Cancel transfer** at the bottom left.

If you initiated the transfer from the **Supplier Invoices** section and it's been **[cancelled](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947726849937)**, **[declined](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947662864785)**, or **[returned](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947694963857)**, the invoice will return to your **Inbox**.

**Delete an invoice:**
----------------------

To delete an invoice, click the three dots "**...**" on the invoice or select the invoice and click the trash can icon 🗑️ in the top right corner.

**Delete a credit note:**
-------------------------

To delete a credit note, click the three dots "**...**" on the credit note or select the invoice and click the trash can icon 🗑️ in the top right corner.

**Find an invoice:**
--------------------

You can filter invoices by supplier, payment status, and alert status using the drop down menus at the top of the page.

You can also use the search bar to perform a text search by supplier name or invoice number.

Click any of the column headers, such as Supplier, Status, or Due date, to sort the invoices by that column.

**Find a credit note:**
-----------------------

In the Supplier Invoices section, click on the **All Invoices** tab, and then click on the **Amount** column header at the top-right to bring up the credit notes first.

**Avoiding Duplicates:**
------------------------

To prevent paying the same supplier invoice twice, a notification will appear during the import of your invoice if our system suspects a duplicate.

In this case, you can click on **Review Invoices** on the notification. A screen will then display the imported invoice along with potential duplicates.

You can then decide whether you want to keep or delete it.

**Manage your suppliers list:**
-------------------------------

You can easily edit or delete your suppliers from the **Supplier list** section.

1. In the **Invoices** section, under **Suppliers**, click on **Supplier list**
2. Find your supplier and click on it, a column will open on the right
3. In the top right corner, click the pen to edit or the bin to delete

☝️ Good to know :You can not modify the IBAN from the **Supplier list**. To do so, you have to go to the Transfer section, see [How to add, edit or delete a beneficiary (SEPA Transfers beneficiary)?](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947670737937-How-to-add-edit-or-delete-a-beneficiary-SEPA-Transfers-beneficiary)
