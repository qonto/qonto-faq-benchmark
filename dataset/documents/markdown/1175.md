# How to make a transfer in euros (SEPA)?

There's nothing easier than processing a transfer!

You can make a transfer directly from your web or mobile app.
With Qonto, you can initiate instant SEPA transfers! How? Just follow the steps below👇

👆**Good to know:** One time, scheduled and recurring transfers are configured by default as an instant transfer but you can also choose to uncheck this parameter and make a classic transfer (received within 48 working hours) in the *One Last Look* step of the flow.

**💻 On your web app:**
----------------------

* Go to the **Business Account** section
* Go to the **Transfers** section
* Click on the black **+ icon** in the top right corner of your screen

  You will have 2 choices :
  -------------------------

  1- Upload an invoice
  --------------------

  If you have an invoice, upload it right now!

  + The system will gather information on the invoice whenever it is possible (beneficiary / iban, amount, reference),
  + You will be able to modify any field, the account to debit, schedule a recurrence, send notification to the beneficiary, add bookeeping details.

  2- I don’t have an invoice / Add transfer details
  -------------------------------------------------

  If you don’t have an invoice, follow this steps:

  + Click on **Add transfer details**
  + Click on **SEPA transfer in euros**
  + Choose the account to debit
  + Choose a beneficiary OR add a new beneficiary: the full name as mentioned on the RIB (last name, first name, name of the company)

    The new beneficiary will be automatically saved
  + Enter the **amount** and **reference** of the transfer (possibility to save up to 135 characters in the reference field).
  + **Programming and recurrence:** possibility to define the date on which the transfer will be executed, or to set a recurrence by selecting **Transfer recurrence**
  + **Additional information (everything is optional):** possibility to add an attachement to the transfer, to select the VAT and analytics label. You can also select \*\*Notify the beneficiary (\*\*possibility to send an automatic email to the beneficiary with the details of the transfer made)
  + **One last look: you** can double check the information of the transfer before validating it

  You will be asked to confirm the operation using their mobile phone.

**📲 On your mobile app:**
-------------------------

* Go to the **Menu >** **Transfers** section
* Click on the black **+ icon** in the top right corner of your screen

You will have 2 choices :
-------------------------

### 1- Upload an invoice (coming soon on mobile)

If you have an invoice, upload it right now!

* The system will gather information on the invoice whenever it is possible (beneficiary/iban, amount, reference),
* You will be able to modify any field, the account to debit, schedule a recurrence, send notification to the beneficiary, add bookeeping details.

Here is how to proceed:

* + Click on **Add an invoice**
  + Take a picture of the invoice or upload it from your mobile
  + Choose the **account to debit**, select a **beneficiary** OR add a **new beneficiary**: the full name as mentioned on the RIB (last name, first name, name of the company). The new beneficiary will be automatically saved.

    - Add a **reference** (visible by the beneficiary).
    - **Programming and recurrence:** possibility to define the date on which the transfer will be executed, or to set a recurrence by selecting **Transfer recurrence.**
  + You can add more **attachment**, choose the **VAT** and **analytics label** if needed (optional)

    - You can also select \*\*Notify the beneficiary (\*\*possibility to send an automatic email to the beneficiary with the details of the transfer)
    - **One last look: you** can double check the information of the transfer before validating it

    You will be asked to confirm the operation using your mobile phone.

### 2- I don’t have an invoice / Add transfer details

If you don’t have an invoice, follow this steps:

* Click on **Add transfer details**
* Click on **SEPA transfer in euros**
* Choose the account to debit
* Choose a beneficiary OR add a new beneficiary: the full name as mentioned on the RIB (last name, first name, name of the company)

  The new beneficiary will be automatically saved
* Enter the **amount** and **reference** of the transfer (possibility to save up to 135 characters in the reference field).
* **Programming and recurrence:** possibility to define the date on which the transfer will be executed, or to set a recurrence by selecting **Transfer recurrence**
* **Additional information (everything is optional):** possibility to add an attachement to the transfer, to select the VAT and analytics label. You can also select **Notify the beneficiary** (possibility to send an automatic email to the beneficiary with the details of the transfer made)
* **One last look: you** can double check the information of the transfer before validating it

☝️ **Good to know** : You don’t need a new beneficiary to be validated before initiating a new transfer.

In order to save time, you can settle your VAT rate and associated label for every single beneficiary, by clicking on **Bookkeeping,** then **Suppliers**. Once it's done, you don't need to think about it anymore! You will find already filled-in the dedicated fields with the information, when you will create a new transfer while you are making it.

Managers may make transfers as their authorizations allow them to, within limits set by the Admin or the Account Holder.

**Transfers between Qonto accounts are not deducted from your plan, and credited instantly!** All you need to do is follow the same procedure as for a traditional transfer, by filling in the bank account details (IBAN) of your beneficiary.
​
At the moment, you **can’t** initiate an instant SEPA for scheduled, requested, recurring and bulk transfer.

If your transfer is greater than €100,000 or your beneficiary cannot be reached via instant SEPA transfers, it will be carried out automatically as a standard transfer.

If you make a transfer to a closed account, you must contact the beneficiary so that their bank can confirm the return of funds to your Qonto account (funds should be automatically returned)

☝️ **Tips**:

To save time on your SEPA transfers, use the link [qonto.new](https://qonto.new/) in the search bar of your web browser. You will be taken directly to the SEPA transfers page.

You can also use the Suppliers Invoice section for your transfers and avoid filling manually all the transfer details.

🔁 You can now re-initiate some transfers that you have already made without having to recreate it again 🤩!

Click on the transfer you want to remake in your "**Transaction**" tab, then on the right side you will see the button **Repeat transfer**.

You will be able to edit some transfer details and then validate it.

💡 If you have set up [Strong Customer Authentication](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947670125329) on your account, you will receive a confirmation request on your associated phone.
