# What documents do I need to open a Qonto account for my Foundation with registered office in Italy?

List of the documents needed to open a business account for your Foundation.

[If you wish to open a Qonto account for your Foundation](https://welcome.qonto.com/signup/select-employees?country=IT&status=registered), you will need to have the following documentation at hand:

### **1. Identity documents**

For the Founder and the ultimate beneficial owners, when identified or easily identifiable, we will need:

* **A valid identification document** chosen from the list provided [here](https://qonto9015-it.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949991315473).

  *💡How to provide a valid digital photo of your ID? Follow the instructions in **[this article](https://qonto9015-it.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949941631121).***

👉 **Please keep in mind that** only the Founder or a person delegated by them can open a Qonto account on behalf of the Foundation (if you need a delegation template, you can download it from [here](https://qonto-assets.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/intercom_faq/IT+-+Delega-Generale.pdf)).

---

### 2. Documents regarding the Foundation

Regarding the Foundation, you will need to provide us with:

* The **proof of regisreation to the RUNTS** (*Registro Unico Nazionale del Terzo Settore);*

👉 We inform you that, from a legal stand point **it's is essential for a Foundation to be registered in the RUNTS**. If your Foundation is not enrolled, **we won't be able to move forward with the account opening.**

Moreover, we will also need the following documents:

* The **deed of corporation**;
* The **statute**;
* The **VAT number** and **tax code certificate**;
* The **declaration of beneficial owners** (you can use the template [here](https://qonto-assets.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/intercom_faq/Dichiarazione+titolari+effettivi.pdf)).
