# How can I order a payment terminal with Qonto?

With [Qonto's mobile payment terminal](https://qonto.com/en/payment-methods/pos), accept payments from your customers wherever you are. You benefit from **highly competitive transaction fees** and receive your funds within 1 business day.

Easily manage your finances with **direct integration** within your Qonto account.

Each terminal comes pre-configured for your account, requiring only charging and powering on to start accepting payments. Head over to [this page](https://qonto.com/en-de/payment-methods/pos/set-up) to find out how to turn it on, charge it, and more.

You can order multiple terminals regardless of your subscription plan.

**How can I order my payment terminal ?**
-----------------------------------------

You can now order you POS directly from your Qonto account (available for Admin and owner). It is possible **only from desktop.**

Here is how:

* Click on **Business Account** > **In person Payments** > **Order payment terminal**
* Select the terminal that you want
* Fill-in the personal information
* Review the order and accept the T&C
* Order confirmed 🎉

**What are the payment capabilities and fees?**
-----------------------------------------------

Terminals accept Visa and Mastercard (no American Express) with a € 100,000 transaction limit.

Fees vary by plan:

* 1,2% for Solo Basic, Smart plan, and Team Essential and Business plan
* 0.8% for Solo Premium and Team Enterprise plan
* 2.6% for non-EU cards

Your first 5 transactions are free regardless of amount - it's recommended to start with a € 1 test transaction.

⚠️ Pre-authorizations or payment in installments are not possible with Qonto POS terminals (for security deposit, split payments for example).

**How do transactions and settlements work?**
---------------------------------------------

📱Process payments through the shortcut → **Tap to Pay** or **Business account** → **Tap to Pay**.

🖥️ Navigate to the section **Business account** → Click on **Tap to Pay**.

Funds are settled the next business day (D+1) via a single transfer combining all transactions, with detailed transaction information included.

Refunds can be processed directly in the app by selecting the specific transaction.

**Are there any restrictions on who can use Qonto’s POS?**
----------------------------------------------------------

Yes. To comply with legal, regulatory, and risk-related requirements from our payment partner, some business activities are not eligible to use Qonto’s in-person payment solutions.

Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:

* **Sales of regulated or banned substances** (e.g. tobacco, CBD, vaping products, pharmaceuticals, or alcohol without proper licensing)
* **Adult entertainment and services** (e.g. massage parlors, strip clubs, escort services, adult content sales)
* **Unregulated or high-risk health and wellness services** (e.g. aesthetic treatments without medical certification, IV therapy, unauthorized supplements)
* **Cash-intensive businesses or those subject to stricter regulatory oversight**
* **Gambling, betting, or lottery-related activities** (including fantasy sports or games of skill with prizes)
* **Crypto, forex, and other high-risk financial services** (e.g. crypto exchanges, forex trading, payday loans)
* **Resale of mobile phones, electronics, or counterfeit goods**

Unable to order our POS? [Payment links](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/32613895529745) might just be your fast and straightforward solution.

Need help? Contact us and we’ll gladly check your eligibility.

**What are the delivery times?**
--------------------------------

Terminals are delivered within 1-5 business days through our dedicated delivery service partner and if you are facing technical issues, it will be handled by Qonto support, who can arrange for a replacement if needed.
