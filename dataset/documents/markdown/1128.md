# What is the Accounts Receivable add-on?

While our standard invoicing solution is free and covers your basic needs , our Accounts Receivable add-on provides advanced capabilities for growing businesses. Available for all Qonto plans, it's designed for those who need more flexibility in document creation, automation, and customization.

**What is included in each version?**
-------------------------------------

| **Standard invoicing tool** | **Account Receivable Add-on** |
| --- | --- |
| 🖥️📱 Unlimited invoices and quotes (in 29 currencies and 6 languages) | 🖥️📱 Unlimited e-signatures for quotes |
| **🖥️📱** Credit notes and deposit invoices | 🖥 Recurring invoices |
| 🖥️📱 Light template customization (logo and colors) | 🖥 Advanced document layout and content customization |
| **🖥️📱** Simple catalog of goods and services | 🖥️📱 Quotes and invoices pre-saved templates (coming soon) |
| 🖥️📱 Client list with public database search | 🖥️📱 Flexible documents formatting with sub-sections and file attachments |
| 🖥️📱 Manual reminders | 🖥️📱 Automated payment reminders |
| 🖥 Import and migration of invoices, clients, and items | 🖥 Real-time sync with external tools |
| 🖥️📱 Built-in payment features | 🖥️📱 Projects |
| 🖥️📱 Automated transaction matching |  |

Note: The 🖥️ and 📱 icons indicate whether features are available on web and/or mobile apps.

And there is more coming - we are constantly adding new features to help you manage your invoicing more efficiently! 🙌

**How much does it cost?**
--------------------------

Starting April 1st, 2025, the Accounts Receivable Add-On became available as an additional paid service. You can choose the billing option that works best for you:

* Monthly billing: €45/month excl. VAT
* Annual billing: €35/month excl. VAT (€420 billed once)

💡 There is currently a time-limited promotional offer available from April 1st to August 31th:

1. Monthly billing offer : €35/month excl. VAT in April, May, June, July and August
2. Annual billing offer : €29/month excl. VAT for one year (€348 billed once), to be activated through your account settings before August 31th

### **How can I manage my add-ons?**

You can manage your add-ons from both the web and mobile app. On web, go to **Settings** > **Billing and invoices** (under Organizations) and click on the desired add-on. On mobile, access **Menu** > **Settings** > **Price plan** (under Organization) and select your add-on. For more information about available add-ons, consult our dedicated help article : [What add-ons are available to complete my plan?](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28602398347537)

**What happens if I remove the add-on?**
----------------------------------------

No worries! You will keep access to all standard features. Your recurring invoices will pause, and customizations will return to standard format. While you can remove the add-on anytime, with both monthly and annual billing, access to advanced features will be removed immediately and no reimbursement for the remaining period.
