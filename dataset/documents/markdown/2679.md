# How to download a proof of international transfer?

It is possible to download a proof of transfer directly from your Qonto interface, in case of request for a proof by a third party, for example.

☝️ Good to know: Downloading a proof of transfer is available to **Account Owner**, Admins, and **Managers** with the [appropriate permissions](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950969601553)**.**

Managers without permissions or Employees will not be able to download proofs of transfer, they must contact an Admin or the Owner.

From your web app 💻:

1. Go to the **Business account** tab
2. Then in the **Transactions** tab
3. Select the transfer
4. Click on **Download proof of payment**

From your mobile app 📱:

1. Go to the **Transactions** section
2. Select the transfer
3. Click on **Download proof of payment**

☝️ **Good to know**: This document can be downloaded **only** **if the transfer has been executed**(there might be a delay after the transfer is completed until the proof of payment is made available due to intermediary banks and payment partners).
