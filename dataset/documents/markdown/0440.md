# How are my Qonto fees calculated?

At Qonto, our mission is to offer you a complete business account, with fair and transparent pricing.

How are additional fees calculated?
-----------------------------------

Depending on your plan, a certain number of operations are included while others will be additional fees.

### Cards

Fees related to [cards](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947610361361) depend on your subscription and the type of card.

* **Physical cards:** Additional cards will be charged (€5 per One card, €8 per Plus card, €20 per X card).
* **Virtual cards:** With the Basic plan, you will be charged €2 per month, per card. All other plans include 50 virtual cards. Any additional cards beyond this limit will incur a charge of €1 per card, per month.
* **Instant cards**: They are only available in the Expense & Spend Management **[add-on](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28479048690705)**, and they are unlimited and free.

### FX fees

A foreign exchange commission will apply for card payments made in currencies other than the euro, based on your card type:

•**One, Virtual, and Instant Cards**: A 2% fee is charged on all non-euro transactions.

•**Plus Card**: A reduced fee of 1% applies.

•**X Card**: Non-euro transactions are completely free, with no foreign exchange commission.

These fees are automatically calculated and added to your transaction at the time of payment. For more details, check your card’s terms and conditions.

### Cash withdrawal

The billing for cash withdrawal will depend on the card you withdraw cash with:

* One card: €2 per withdrawal;
* Plus card: 5 withdrawals included per month, €2 per additional withdrawal;
* X card: unlimited free withdrawals.

### Transfers and direct debits

* **SEPA zone:** Your plan contains a specific number of transfers (incoming and outgoing) and direct debits. For any additional operation, you will be charged:
  + €0.40 for Basic and Smart
  + €0.25 for Essential and Business
  + €0.10 for Premium and Enterprise
* **International transfers in foreign currencies:** With Qonto, you can make **foreign currency transfers** through our partner Wise, which enables you to process it using local networks, in addition to the SWIFT network. You will find all fees applied in this dedicated article : **[How can I submit international transfers in foreign currencies?](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26049704105361-How-can-I-submit-international-transfers-in-foreign-currencies)**

* **Direct debit collection :** You can collect payments from your client via direct debit. Find all fees applied in the dedicated article : [**How to collect money from my clients?**](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/25012107576209-How-to-collect-money-from-my-clients#h_1a87d44f5b)

### Checks (for French companies only)

Each plan includes a specific number of checks. Additional deposited checks are charged €2.

### Members

* With a Basic, Smart or Premium plan, you can add an Admin role for €10/month.
* With a Smart or Premium plan, you can add unlimited Accountant roles, free of charge.
* With an Essential, Business or Enterprise plan, all additional members (Employee, Manager, Admin and Accountant roles), are free and unlimited.

### Service fees

These additional fees can include the delivery of a new card (€6 excluding VAT) or the processing of irregularities (for French companies), in the context of a judicial authority demanding the repayment of an unpaid debt.

* **SATD**: the management of SATD is invoiced at 10% of the total amount requested by the seizing structure, with a high ceiling of €100.
* **Seizures**: the management of seizures is invoiced at €100.

How to find the details of my Qonto fees?
-----------------------------------------

Moreover, if you wish to see the details of your charges during the current month, you can go to **Settings > Billing and invoice >** **Price plan details**.



You can get additional information on all Qonto fees in your history view by clicking on the transaction line.
