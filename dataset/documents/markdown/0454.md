# What is Qonto Invoicing and how do I get started?

Welcome to Qonto Invoicing ! Your accountant uses Regate by Qonto and has invited you to join forces with them on our platform.

Look out for our welcome email – setting up your account is quick and easy. Just enter your details and your company information, and you will be ready to go in 3 minutes flat. Let's get started!

**What can I do with Qonto Invoicing?**
---------------------------------------

* Upload invoices with one click
* Create sales invoices
* Create personal expense reports
* Monitor your cash flow
* Automatically share this data with your accountant

**How does it improve collaboration with my accountant?**
---------------------------------------------------------

Say goodbye to emails and office visits! Your documents sync instantly with your accountant as soon as you add them to Qonto Invoicing. It's that simple – real-time collaboration without the back-and-forth. Your accounting stays up-to-date automatically, no reminders needed.

**How do I get started with Qonto Invoicing?**
----------------------------------------------

Follow these 3 simple steps:

1. Two possibilities to connect your bank accounts: **Transactions** section > Click on **Transactions** > Select **Connect external account** **Transactions** section > Click on **Accounts** > Select **Add account**
2. Upload supporting documents from: **Transactions** section > Click on **Transactions** > Select **Supporting Documents** by linking them to bank transactions **Invoices** section > Click on **Supplier Invoices** > Select **Import Invoices**
3. Create sales invoices from > **Invoices** section > Click on **Client Invoices >** Select **Create Invoice**
4. Your accountant receives this data in their workspace and processes the accounting without manual sending required

**Can I use Qonto Invoicing on the go?**
----------------------------------------

Yes! Download the Qonto mobile app from the Play Store or App Store. It offers the same features, allowing you to share receipts in real-time from anywhere!

**What about e-invoicing compliance?**
--------------------------------------

The e-invoicing reform will be implemented very soon. Using Qonto Invoicing will allow you to register for free with Qonto PDP (Partner Dematerialization Platform). You will be kept informed of updates!

**What are the key benefits of Qonto Invoicing?**
-------------------------------------------------

* No more back-and-forth emails with your accountant to send documents
* Everything uploaded to Qonto Invoicing is directly synchronized with your accounting firm
* All-in-one tool: document management (invoice upload), billing tool, cash flow visibility
* Simplified document upload: one-click addition, automatic synchronization with your accountant
* Easier cash flow monitoring by linking documents to transactions
* Multiple upload methods: mobile app, web app, email address
* Documents handled: supplier invoices, receipts, personal expense reports, customer invoices, bank transactions
* Your accounting firm receives documents for bookkeeping, enabling faster financial reporting
