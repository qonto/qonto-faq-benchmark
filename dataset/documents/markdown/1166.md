# Who is our insurance partner, SureIn?

SureIn is a centralized insurance platform designed to simplify insurance management for businesses. It offers a digital solution for managing various types of insurance and provides companies with clarity and control over their policies.

With SureIn, you benefit from **efficient insurance management that saves you time and money.**

Who can use SureIn?
-------------------

Whether you run a **digital startup, a construction company, or a retail business**, SureIn offers insurance plans tailored to **various business activities and industries**.

What insurance products does SureIn offer?
------------------------------------------

SureIn offers you the following insurance products:

**Business liability insurance**Business liability insurance protects companies from financial damages arising from **personal, property, or financial damages** caused in the course of business activities, by employees, or on the business premises. It covers third-party claims and simultaneously defends against unjustified claims.

**Cyber insurance**

Cyber insurance protects businesses from the **financial damages caused by cyberattacks or IT security incidents**. This includes data breaches, hacker attacks, ransomware attacks, and IT system failures. It also covers the costs of response and recovery after an incident.

**Financial Loss Liability Insurance**
Professional indemnity insurance protects companies and individuals from **financial damages caused by errors or breaches of duty in the performance of their professional activities**. It is particularly important for consulting, auditing, and planning professions where financial losses are not covered by business liability insurance.

**Legal business insurance**Commercial legal protection insurance, often also referred to as **business legal protection insurance or corporate legal protection**, is a form of coverage that supports businesses in managing the costs associated with legal matters.

How to purchase an insurance with SureIn?
-----------------------------------------

When you select an insurance offer in the Qonto insurance section **(Insurance > *select desired insurance* > get quote)**, you will be redirected to the SureIn platform. There, you must give your consent and can either directly choose a suitable insurance plan for your company or be contacted by a SureIn employee for individual advice. The exact process may vary depending on the selected insurance offer.

What information does SureIn need to create a personalized insurance offer?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

To offer a tailored insurance solution, SureIN usually asks for details about your business activities, company size, and specific risks associated with your industry. This information helps connect you with the most suitable insurance products and plans.
