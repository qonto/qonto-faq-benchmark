# How can I contact Qonto's legal department?

Qonto has two pec addresses dedicated to authorities, to be distinguished according to the nature of the contact. Specifically:

* If you need to share with our legal service significant changes in the company such as **bankruptcies, foreclosures, liquidations**, **abandonment of pending proceedings** invite your **attorney, or bankruptcy trustee or judicial commissioner** to write to this address: **backoffice.olindasas@legalmail.it**;

* Regarding **criminal police investigations and inquiries, seizures or complaints**, law enforcement agencies can contact us by sending a pec to this address: **rich.autorita@legalmail.it.**

  **N.B.** Any request not relevant to these two offices will not be taken.

👉 **Any other questions or inquiries should instead be directed to our customer service department** via chat directly from your Qonto interface or by logging into our [Help Center](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us) and clicking on the purple button at the bottom right.
