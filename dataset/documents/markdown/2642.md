# What are the different Qonto cards ?

Qonto provides physical and virtual Mastercard debit cards (you can have [up to 5 of each if desired](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951037384593)).

Qonto physical cards
====================

Our **physical cards** can be used **immediately** after creation for all kinds of payments, in-store or online ⚡️ More infos [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950925341201).

According to your subscription, it will include one or several free ONE cards. Check what your subscription includes [right here](https://qonto.com/fr/pricing?select-toggle=creation&periodicity=yearly).

You can follow the steps to order your card [right here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951014900881).

**One Card**
------------

Free according to your subscription or 5€/month (excl. tax)

* Mastercard Business Debit
* Pay up to €20,000 per calendar month
* Withdraw up to €1,000 per calendar month
* Unlimited and free payment in Europe
* 2€ (excl. VAT) / withdrawal
* 2% foreign exchange commission on non-euro payments (and no fixed fees)
* Produced from recycled ocean plastic

**Plus Card**
-------------

€8 / month (excl. tax)

* Mastercard World Debit
* Color customization: silver, black or purple
* Choose between embossed or printed cards
* Pay up to €40,000 per calendar month
* Withdraw up to €2,000 per calendar month
* Unlimited and free payment in Europe
* 5 free withdrawals then 2€ (excl. VAT) / withdrawal
* 1% foreign exchange commission on non-euro payments (and no fixed fees)
* Benefit from advanced card insurance. More info [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950925916689-How-does-my-card-insurance-work)
* Produced from recycled ocean plastic

**X Card**
----------

€20 / month (excl. tax)

* Mastercard World Debit
* Color customization: black, silver or gold
* Benefit from a 0% foreign exchange commission on non-euro payments (and no fixed fees)
* Pay up to

  + €200,000 per calendar month **if you are enrolled SCA** (Strong Customer Authentication)
  + €60 000 per month **if you haven’t activate your SCA**. We strongly advise you to activate this strong authentification, following these steps : **[How to pair my phone with my Qonto account?](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950992516113)**
* Withdraw up to €3,000 for free, per calendar month

  (💡 If you are registered to a German account, you can benefit from higher withdrawal limits.)
* Take full advantage of a concierge service available 7 days a week
* Access airport lounges
* Benefit from Qonto’s most exclusive card insurance. More info [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950925916689)

☝️ Good to know: To confirm the order of an X card, you need to first top-up your Qonto account through a simple transfer. Find how to do it right [here](https://support.qonto.eu/hc/en-us/articles/115000517089-How-can-I-make-a-transfer-to-my-Qonto-account-).

Qonto virtual cards
-------------------

[The virtual card](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950913755409) can be included in your package or charged 2€ (excl. tax) / month. They allow you to make online payments up to €20,000 per calendar month.

Find out more about it [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950913755409)

---

Qonto Online advertising cards
------------------------------

Online advertising card are **virtual cards** specifically designed to help you manage your marketing campaigns across top ads platforms and merchants like Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and other.

Find out more about it [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950932465169)

---

Qonto Instant cards
-------------------

[Instant cards](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950994891025) are temporary virtual cards. They are ideal for teams’ one-off expenses. No need to share a card or pay out-of-pocket!

Find out more about it [here](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950994891025)

---

Qonto Fuel cards
----------------

Qonto does not offer a fuel card per se. However, you can easily order a physical card and restrict its use solely to **gas stations** from the ***specific spending category* section**. You can also define the ***days it can be used*** and set a ***payment limit*** according to your needs. These options are available only for the Essential, Business, and Enterprise plans.
