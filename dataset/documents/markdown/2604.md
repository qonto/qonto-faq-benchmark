# What is the difference between a SWIFT transfer and a SEPA transfer?

The **[SEPA](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950966759825)** [network](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950966759825) (Single Euro Payment Area) makes it possible to send euros within the European Union. This network also includes the 4 EFTA countries (Iceland, Norway, Liechtenstein, Switzerland), 4 microstates (Andorra, Monaco, San Marino, Vatican), and the United Kingdom.

The **SWIFT** network (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication) makes it possible to send single non-euro transfers anywhere in the world. They are also referred to as “non-SEPA zone” transfers.

This means that you can [send transfers](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951030976017) and also [receive transfers](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951029272081), to/from many countries in the world and in several currencies.

Unlike with the SEPA network, institutions must pay to access the SWIFT network.

Qonto is connected to both **SEPA** and **SWIFT** networks.
