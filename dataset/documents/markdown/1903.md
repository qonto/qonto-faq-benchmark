# Qonto cards payment and withdrawal limits

Qonto cards are personal and can be adapted to all member needs. The Admin of the Qonto account or the Manager (for their team) can limit the card payment and withdrawal capabilities of the members of his company.

For more flexibility, these limits can be modified at any time from the section **‘Business Account’,** click on **‘Cards’**. Any changes will be taken into account in real-time.

Choose a card that suits your needs
===================================

With a **One card**:

* Pay up to €20,000 and withdraw up to €1,000 per calendar month

With a **Plus card:**

* Pay up to €40,000 and withdraw up to €2,000 per calendar month

With an **X card**:

* Pay up to

  + €200,000 per calendar month **if you are enrolled SCA** ([Strong Customer Authentication](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949944205201))
  + €60 000 per month **if you haven’t activate your SCA**. We strongly advise you to activate this strong authentification, following these steps : **[How to pair my phone with my Qonto account?](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949957227025)**

* Withdraw up to 3 000€ / month
  (💡If you are registered to a german account, you can benefit from higher withdrawal limits. Please klick [here](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/6432685-additional-information-about-x-card-withdrawal-limits) for more information)

👉 Once you receive your physical card, using your PIN code for the first time (physical payment or withdraw) will allow to fully activate your card in a secured way :

* The contactless payments
* The monthly payment limits above 20,000€ for PLUS and X card

How to change the payment and withdrawal limits of a card?
==========================================================

> ⚠️ The maximum payment and withdrawal limits thresholds for cards are set and can not be changed or adjusted.

**📚 If required, the payment limits can be reduced from the maximum allowed limits.**

They include:

* Monthly limit
* Daily limit
* Per-transaction limit

### During the card creation

* **From the section Business Account,** **click on** **Cards**, click on **Create card**
* Choose between a physical card, virtual or Instant card
* Then choose the payment and withdrawal limits of the new card in the **Card settings** section

### After the card creation

* Select the card that you want to change the limits in the **Cards** section
* Click on **Update** in card settings
* Then, update your limits in the following window

👉 Other options can be set up on your card. For more information, [click here](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950014120977).
