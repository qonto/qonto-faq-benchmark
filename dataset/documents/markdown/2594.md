# Where do I find my statements?

Statement

You can access your account statements at any time from your Qonto interface, either on our website or on our mobile application!

💡 Account statements are only available in PDF format.

🖥️ Web app :
============

To obtain your statements, go to the **Business account** section of your web application, then in the **Accounts** section, click on **Download account statements**. Select the desired statement(s) based on the account (main account or subaccount if applicable) and the desired monthly period.

Click on [this link](https://app.qonto.com?tdm=download-bank-statement) to view the demo in your own interface.





📲 Mobile app:
=============

By clicking on the **More** tab, you will find your documents in the **Account Statements** section.

Your account's statement is not available yet?
==============================================

Account statements are generally generated in the first few days of the month. In the meantime, in case you require something close to a statement, you can create a transaction export in a few minutes. To proceed, you need to:

1. Visit the **Transactions** tab (in the Business account section)
2. Filter the transactions by settlement date
3. Click on **Export transactions**
4. Select **Simple data export or create a custom export template by adding the following columns:**

   1. Settlement date (UTC)
   2. Counterparty name
   3. Debit
   4. Credit

💡 Transaction exports are available in Excel, CSV, OFX or QIF format. Find further details on [this link](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951013272593-How-can-I-export-my-transactions).

For more information on **How to use custom exports,** please read [this article](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950922077457).

Having trouble understanding the balance of your statement?
===========================================================

For accounting reasons, your statements only take account of **Settled** transactions and do not include **Processing** operations.

If you want more details on the **Settled** or **Processing** operations, we invite you to click on your account balance on the left side on your interface.

The balance that is displayed on your interface is called **"authorized balance"**. It **takes into account all transactions**, whether they are marked as **Settled** or **Processing**, so you always know what amount remains available to you.

We do not count upcoming supplier payments in the calculation.
