# How to automate the processing of my receipts with Qonto?

Automatically associate all your invoices and receipts to the right transactions in Qonto and save valuable time on your bookkeeping.

👉 Customer invoices can only be [manually](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947723392273) associated to your transactions from your **Business account > Transactions**.

How does the automatic processing of receipts work?
---------------------------------------------------

Qonto can process any supplier invoice or receipt imported from your **[Supplier Invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947625621777)** section, transferred from your [email box](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947591709457) or imported from your online storage space, [Google Drive](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947658434705), [Dropbox](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947628575505), or Supplier portal.

When imported, invoices are automatically scanned by Qonto. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) extracts all the important information contained in your document, and our algorithm analyzes this information to associate the invoice with one of your outgoing payments.

💡 We even automatically extract the [VAT](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947700215569) for you!

How to import a supplier invoice or a receipt in Qonto?
-------------------------------------------------------

Import all your receipts, regardless of their origin:

1. Use your **[Supplier Invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947625621777)** section to download multiple invoices at once, either paid or unpaid.
2. Forward your invoices received by email to [your forwarding email address](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947591709457). You can even [automate the transfer](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947609661969) of your digital receipts from Gmail to Qonto.
3. Connect your cloud storage solution directly to your Qonto account, in order to automatically import all your invoices and receipts from your [Google Drive](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947658434705) or [Dropbox](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947628575505) folder (available on 💻desktop).
4. The **Get My Invoices** feature enables you to connect to over 10,000 different supplier portals

   1. From the **Integration and Partnership‘s** section (only on 💻desktop):

      1. 1. click on **Featured**
         2. search for the supplier portal you would like to connect to, select it
         3. click on **Connect**.
      2. From the **Invoices** section (on 💻 desktop):
         1. click on **Supplier Invoices**
         2. press the **Activate integrations and tools** button in the top right corner 🔌
         3. select **Import receipts and invoices**
         4. search for the supplier portal you would like to connect to, click on **Connect**.
      3. Once you have clicked on Connect, it will take a few seconds before the set up begins. Then, you will be able to:
         1. enter your supplier **login** details
         2. decide on the **starting date** to import your already existing invoices
         3. click on **Finish/Save**.

If you can’t find the supplier integration you’re looking for, please feel free to [fill in this form](https://qonto.typeform.com/to/OwhVDwQz).

Invoices and receipts added [manually](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947723392273) to a transaction from the **Business account** section will not appear in your **Supplier invoices** section.

What if my receipt was not automatically matched to the right transaction?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

If no corresponding transaction can be found, you can attach the invoices to the right transactions manually:

1. Click on the **Invoices** menu and then access your **Supplier invoices** section.
2. In the **Imported** tab, visualize all the invoices that could not be automatically attached to a transaction, sorted by due date.
3. Click on the invoice you wish to link to an existing transaction, and then on the **Mark as paid** button.
4. Select the transaction you wish to associate your receipt with, or click on **Continue without transaction** if you don't wish to associate it with any.

💡 Good to know: Unpaid invoices also appear on the **Imported** tab of your **[Supplier Invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947625621777)** section, to help you streamline the management and payment of your upcoming payments. [(Read more)](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947625621777)

If your invoice or receipt is neither accessible from the **Imported** tab nor the **Processing** tab of your **Supplier Invoices** section, please verify that your document does not exceed 15 MB and that all the important information on your invoice is legible (e.g. VAT number, amount, etc.). You can then try to re-import the invoice or download it directly in your **Business account >Transactions** by clicking on the corresponding transaction.
