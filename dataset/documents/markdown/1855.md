# How to request an electronic signature on a quote?

### **What is Electronic Signature for Quotes?**

Electronic signature is a secure and legally compliant way to get your quotes signed digitally. It's an integrated solution that allows for one-click quote acceptance, eliminating the need for manual PDF downloads and signatures.

The Electronic Signature feature is available to all **Admin** and **Owner** roles who have subscribed to the **Account Receivable Add-on**.

You can access and use the feature seamlessly across both **web** and **mobile platforms**, ensuring flexibility in managing your quote signatures wherever you are.

### **How do I request an Electronic Signature for my quotes?**

Here's the simple step-by-step process:

1. Create or select your quote in Qonto
2. Click on the signature setup option
3. Enter the required information:
   * Recipient's email address
   * Recipient's first and last name
   * Choose whether you want to countersign the quote
4. Add a personalized message (optional)
5. Review and send



### **What happens after I send a quote for signature?**

The process continues as follows:

* Your client receives a secure email link
* They can sign with just one click, no account needed
* You get notified when the quote is viewed and signed
* The quote is automatically marked as approved once signed
* You can track the status in your dashboard at any time

### **What information will my client need to sign?**

Your client needs minimal information:

* Just their email address to receive the signing link
* No Qonto account required
* No additional software to download
* No complex identity verification
* No registration needed



### **How do I track my quote's signature status?**

You can easily monitor your quotes:

* Check real-time status in your dashboard
* See when quotes are sent, viewed, and signed
* Get automatic notifications at each step
* Convert signed quotes to invoices with one click
* Access a complete history of all signature activities

### **What are the benefits of using Electronic Signatures?**

Key advantages include:

* Faster signature process with one-click acceptance
* No need to juggle multiple tools
* Centralized quote management and tracking
* Automatic status updates
* Legally compliant signatures
* Time savings compared to manual PDF handling

### **Is the Electronic Signature legally valid?**

Yes, the solution uses YouSign, which is eIDAS-certified. It provides Simple Electronic Signature (SES) capabilities, which are sufficient for quote signing and fully compliant with European regulations.
