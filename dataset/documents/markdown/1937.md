# What are online advertising cards and how do they work?

Qonto has received an order from Bank of Italy to enhance its anti-money laundering measures in its Italian branch. For this reason the subscription of new debit cards is temporarily suspended. If you already have a Digital Advertising card and are wondering how to use it, please continue reading this article.

Online advertising cards are **virtual cards** designed specifically to help you manage your marketing campaigns across top ads platforms and merchants like Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and others.
​
With online advertising cards, you won’t have to worry about your campaigns getting blocked because you’ve run out of funds or exceeded your spending cap - all thanks to higher limits (€200,000/month, or more upon request and approval\*), flexible controls, and efficient delegation options for your team.

**At which platforms and merchants can I use it?**
--------------------------------------------------

You can set up your card as a payment method at most of the top leading ads platforms that you already work with:

* Google Ads
* Facebook Ads,
* Instagram Ads
* TikTok Ads
* Snapchat Ads
* LinkedIn Ads
* Pinterest Ads
* Microsoft Advertising
* Amazon Ads
* Apple Search Ads
* Twitter Ads
* and Leboncoin Publicité
  ​

To use your card, just click on **Go to Cards section** on the success screen or locate your card directly in the **Cards** section and then, click on **Reveal** on the side panel. From there, you’ll be able to copy your card’s details and securely add it as a payment method to your Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and other ads management platforms.

To keep things organized, you can also assign your card a unique name (e.g. Facebook Ads). To do so, go to **Cards**, click on the card that you want to name, and go to **Details** to assign it a name.

**Can I change my card’s limits after I’ve created it?**
--------------------------------------------------------

Absolutely! Just select your card in the **Cards** section and click on the edit icon in the card settings. Then, simply update the limits and confirm the changes.

**Can I request a monthly limit of over €200,000?**
---------------------------------------------------

You can request a limit of up to €1,000,000 per month per card, subject to eligibility and approval from Qonto. Learn more about eligibility criteria [here](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949895170065).
