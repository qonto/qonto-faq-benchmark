# What is the difference between Qonto and a traditional bank account?

Payment institutions and banks

Qonto is a **payment institution supervised by the Banque de France** (under number CIB 16958).

We allow existing companies as well as companies that are under the registration process to benefit from **a current account** and **all the means of payment they need** to run their activity efficiently :

* Debit cards (physical, virtual, and instant cards)
* Instant transfers
* International transfers
* Check deposit (only to companies registered in France)

However, these are the main differences between your Qonto account and a traditional business account:

1. Your Qonto account cannot be overdrawn.
2. We do not allow cash deposits.
3. We provide loans through our partners. Know more about our offers, available through the financing section, in [this article](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949197790609).
4. With Qonto, you have all your finance needs safely organized under one roof, whether you’re alone or leading a team.
