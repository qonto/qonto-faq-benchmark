# Where do I find my statements?

You can access your account statements at any time through your Qonto interface, whether on our website or mobile application!

Note: Account statements are only available in PDF format.

Web application:
----------------

To obtain your statements, go to the **"Business account"** section of your web application, then in the **"Accounts"** section, click on **"Download account statements"**. Select the desired statement(s) based on the account (main account or subaccount if applicable) and the desired monthly period.

Click on this link to view the demo in your own interface: [https://app.qonto.com?tdm=download-bank-statement](https://app.qonto.com/?tdm=download-bank-statement)





Mobile application:
-------------------

Go to **"Menu"** at the bottom right of your screen, then to **"Accounts (IBAN)"**, select the desired account, then click on the **"Account statements"** button.

Your account statement is not available yet?
--------------------------------------------

Account statements are usually generated in the first few days of the month. Meanwhile, if you need a document similar to an account statement, you can create a transaction export in just a few minutes. Here’s how to do it:

1. From your web application, go to the **"Transactions"** section.
2. Filter transactions by settlement date. Click on **"Export transactions"**.
3. Select **"Simple data export"** or create a custom export template by adding the following columns:
   * Settlement Date (UTC)
   * Beneficiary Name
   * Debit
   * Credit

Note: Transaction exports are available in Excel, CSV, OFX or QIF format. Find further details [here](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947723221521).

For more information on using custom exports, please refer to this [article](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947593685009).

Having trouble understanding the balance of your account statement?
-------------------------------------------------------------------

For accounting reasons, your bank statements only account for "**Settled**" transactions. "**Pending**" operations are not included.

If you want more details about "**Settled**" or "**Pending**" operations, we invite you to click on the balance of your account on the left side of your interface.

The balance displayed on your interface is called the **"Available balance"**. It considers all transactions, whether marked as "**Settled**" or "**Pending**", so you always know the amount available to you.

Upcoming payments from suppliers are not included in the calculation of the available balance.
