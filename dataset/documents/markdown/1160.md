**How much does the Expense & Spend Management add-on cost?**
-------------------------------------------------------------

The add-on is available for €89/month with monthly billing or €69/month with annual billing (excluding VAT).

It is available for Team plans (Essential, Business, and Enterprise).

**What is an approval workflow?**
---------------------------------

An [approval workflow](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26624250395921-How-to-customize-approval-workflow-for-transfer-reimbursement-requests-and-supplier-invoices) is a process that manages spending requests from initiation to final decision.

This feature is available through the **Account Payable** and **Expense & Spend Management** [add-ons](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28509717293713-What-add-ons-are-available-to-complement-my-plan).

**How does budget management work?**
------------------------------------

* Set [budgets](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950884922513-Creating-team-budgets-on-Qonto) for each team
* Create dedicated accounts with specific IBANs
* Track spending by team, project, or expense category
* Monitor real-time budget usage.

### **How can I manage my add-ons?**

You can manage your add-ons from both the web and mobile app. On web, go to **Settings** > **Billing and invoices** (under Organizations) and click on the desired add-on. On mobile, access **Menu** > **Settings** > **Price plan** (under Organization) and select your add-on. For more information about available add-ons, consult our dedicated help article: [What add-ons are available to complete my plan?](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28509717293713-What-add-ons-are-available-to-complement-my-plan)

**What happens if I remove the add-on?**
----------------------------------------

No worries! You will keep access to all standard features. Your recurring invoices will pause, and customizations will return to standard format. While you can remove the add-on anytime with monthly billing, annual billing features remain active until the end of your billing period, with no mid-term reimbursement.
