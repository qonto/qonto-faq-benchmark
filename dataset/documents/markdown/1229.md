# How can I update my phone number?

You can change your phone number at any time by accessing your Qonto application **from your desktop or from your mobile application**. To confirm this update, we need to verify your identity.

### **I have access to my account and I want to change my phone number, what should I do?**

**From a computer**

1. Log in to your Qonto account
2. Go to **Settings** in the bottom left corner and click on **Personal settings > Personal profile.**
3. Click on the **Change** button - bellow your previous the phone number
4. Enter your new phone number
5. Enter the confirmation code sent on the new phone number
   * If you have configured the [Strong Customer Authentication](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947670125329), you will receive a confirmation request on your associated phone.
   * If you have not yet configured the Strong Customer Authentication, you can choose to receive the confirmation code on your previous number or by email. Enter the confirmation code sent to your previous phone number or to your email.
6. Your phone number is changed!

**From the mobile application**

1. Log in to your Qonto account
2. Go to the **Menu** tab of your app, click on ⚙️ and finally click on your profile picture
3. Click on the pencil to the right of the phone number
4. Enter your new phone number
5. Enter the confirmation code sent on the new phone number

   * If you have configured the [Strong Customer Authentication](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947670125329), you will receive a confirmation request on your associated phone
   * If you have not yet configured the Strong Customer Authentication, enter the confirmation code sent to your previous phone number
6. Your phone number is changed!

### **You can’t modify your phone number or an error message is displayed?**

Please contact us, we'll be thrilled to help you ! 
First, we will need to verify your identity to ensure the security of your account. Therefore, you can send us the following elements by chat or email :

* A **selfie**, having in your hand your ID document close to your face, and a blank sheet of paper with the current date and the word **QONTO** handwritten.

  
* The following information:

  1. Company's name
  2. SIREN
  3. Last name
  4. Name
  5. New phone number

### **Why is it important to keep your phone number updated?**

There are **three cases** for which your phone number is required:

* It allows us to **quickly get in touch** with you if we **suspect fraudulent use** of your account
* It will be asked in order to **set up the [2FA verification](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947714708881)**.
* It **makes [3-D Secure protocol](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947671680529) possible** (i.e. the sending of a confirmation code for online purchases).

Make sure to keep your contact details in your user profile up-to-date so we can stay connected. 🙌
