# What permissions does the Manager role have?

The manager role is divided into two levels of permissions:

* Basic Manager : available with Business and Enterprise plans.
* Advanced Manager : available through [*add-ons Account Payable and Expense & Spend Management*.](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28602398347537-What-add-ons-are-available-to-complete-my-plan)

The role of the manager is also customizable: Admins or Account Holder define permissions for each manager individually.

**What is the scope of permissions for a Basic and Advanced Manager?**
======================================================================

Depending on how much they want to delegate, the Admin or the Account Holder defines the scope of permissions for each manager:

* on the **whole company** level
* or on a **team** level

💡 You need to [create a team](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/5685161-how-can-i-create-and-edit-a-team-on-qonto) before assigning a Manager to a team.

|  | **Basic Manager** | **Advanced Manager** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access transactions | ✅ | ✅ |
| Make payments from selected accounts | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage cards | ✅ | ✅ |
| Make transfers and reimburse expenses (Set spending limits, per month and per transfer) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Upload and set up supplier invoices | ✅ | ✅ |
| Invite team members as Employees | ✅ | ✅ |
| Invite team members as Employees | ✅ | ✅ |
| Review card requests | ✅ | ✅ |
| View selected account balances | ❌ | ✅ |
| View IBAN and download statements | ❌ | ✅ (Only available for Advanced Managers on **whole company level)** |
| Manage bookkeeping and suppliers | ❌ | ✅ |
| Approve transfers and reimburse expenses including mileage | ❌ | ✅ |
| Set up and suspend direct debit mandates | ❌ | ✅ |
| Deposit checks | ❌ | ✅ |

Managers can also be part of an approval workflow that the Account Holder or Admin will customize. If you have the Account payable or Expense & Spend management add-ons, please visit [How to customize workflow approval for transfer and reimbursement requests?](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/26057885522193-How-to-customize-workflow-approval-for-transfer-and-reimbursement-requests) to find out more about multi-layer approval workflows to keep control over big amounts to reimburse or pay with configurable approvals.

**Can a Manager access certain sub-accounts?**
==============================================

Yes, whether the permissions of a Manager are on a team or a whole company level, you can attribute one or several sub accounts to these roles. They will be able to access transactions and make payments from the selected accounts.

* The Manager already has a profil on the account : In that case, go to **User Management** > **User Access** > **Active** > click on the particular Manager you want to edit > **Account Permissions** > click on the **pencil** next to **View transactions and make payments from selected accounts** to **select the sub-accounts** they can access.
* The Manager has not been invited on the account yet : Go to **User Management** > **User Access** > **Invite a member** > follow the inscription steps and **select the sub-accounts** they can access.

**What permissions can’t be delegated to a Manager?**
=====================================================

❌ Invite all types of team members: Admins, Managers, and Accountants

❌ Change roles status for themselves and other users

❌ Edit subscription

❌ Set up multi-accounts

❌ Set up and manage third-party tools via Connect integrations

💡 Admins can do these actions.

How to check the **permissions of a Manager?**
==============================================

It is possible to view the scope and permissions of a manager directly from their member profile.

From a computer:

1. Go to **User management,**
2. Click on **User access,**
3. Select the profile of the Manager you wish to view.

From the mobile app:

1. Go to **Menu** > settings symbol “⚙️” > **User management**
2. Click on the "plus" symbol at the top right of the screen.
