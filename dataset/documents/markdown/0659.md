# How can I update my personal and business information?

For [legal](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGIARTI000036824668/2018-10-01) and security reasons, maintaining up-to-date personal and professional information is crucial.

**👆 Good to know:** These updates must be made via a computer (web app).

👤 Updating personal information
-------------------------------

1. Access your **Web App** and navigate to "**Settings**" (located in the bottom left corner) > "**Organization details"** > "**Company profile**".
2. Click the pencil symbol button next to your personal profile to edit your information.
3. In the section "**Submit an up-to-date proof of identity**", attach a document justifying the information change. 💡 Check out the [identity documents accepted by Qonto](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947696072721).
4. After updating, click on "**Save"**.

🏢 Updating my business information
----------------------------------

**Note:** This update is only applicable to companies. Associations should contact Support at [support@qonto.com](mailto:support@qonto.com) for address changes.

1. In the section "**File an up-to-date registration document**", include a document such as a KBIS, INSEE's "Avis de situation," INPI's "Document de synthèse définitive," or INPI's "extrait d'immatriculation" **dated within the last 3 months**.
2. You may also upload a new company logo or update the billing email address independently.
3. Click on **Save** to apply the updates.

👥 Update of company leaders and shareholders.
---------------------------------------------

If you are the corporate officer and legal representative of the company and this is not reflected in the records, please update accordingly:



1. Select "**Yes**"
2. Submit an up-to-date registration document.
3. Ensure your tax information is filled in correctly.
4. **Save** the changes and confirm that your business information is up-to-date.
5. Our teams will then check and validate the new information. ✅

Important: Only the **Account Holder** and **Admins** can modify information. If you do not have these permissions, please contact us for assistance.

For additional changes such as updating your phone number or email address, please refer to:
