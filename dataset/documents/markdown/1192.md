# How to get a free one-month trial for Qonto add-ons?

[Add-ons](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28479048690705-What-add-ons-are-available-to-complete-my-plan) offer a set of advanced features and can be subscribed to on top of a price plan. They enhance your account with extra functionalities to better suit your business needs.

⚠️ No add-ons are included in any pricing plan by default.

The current add-ons available are:

* [**Expense & Spend Management**](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/35194398200721): Available for Team plans only (Essential, Business, Enterprise).
* [**Accounts Payable**](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/34968670812049): Available for Team plans only (Essential, Business, Enterprise).
* [**Accounts Receivable**](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/34842220945041): Available for all plans.
* [**Cash Flow Management**](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/35544335925009): Available for all plans

Add-ons are available for all countries and can be activated by Owners or Admins, depending on the current pricing plan you have. [Certain add-ons are only available for Team plans.](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?periodicity=yearly&select-toggle=team&website_addon_period=yearly#:~:text=Customize%20your%20plan%20with%20Add%2Dons)

How does it work?
-----------------

We offer a one-month free trial for all add-ons. Here are the main rules:

1. **The Free Trial is Customer-Initiated**
   * You must activate the free trial yourself.
2. **Duration and Activation**
   * The free trial lasts for one month.
   * You can activate multiple add-on free trials simultaneously.
   * You can start a free trial for an add-on even if another add-on free trial is already active.
3. **Eligibility**
   * The free trial can only be activated after completing your initial Qonto plan trial.
   * It is only available if you have never subscribed to that specific add-on before.
4. **Billing Cycles**
   * The free trial applies to both monthly and annual billing.
   * During the trial, you can switch the billing cycle (monthly or annual) for the add-on.
   * If you activate a free trial through an upsell entry point, the billing cycle defaults to monthly. For solo plan users, upgrading your plan may be necessary to access certain add-ons.
   * If you don’t cancel the add-on before the trial ends, you will be billed for the add-on alongside your current billing cycle.

Can I cancel the free trial?
----------------------------

Yes, you can at any time. However, if you activate an add-on trial and decide it’s not the right time, you won’t be able to activate the free trial that add-on again.

How does the add-on free trial Impact my Qonto plan free trial?
---------------------------------------------------------------

If you start an add-on free trial that requires a plan upgrade, Qonto will automatically offer you a free trial for the required plan.

For example: to use **Accounts Payable** or **Expense & Spend Management**, you will need to upgrade to the Essential plan or higher.
