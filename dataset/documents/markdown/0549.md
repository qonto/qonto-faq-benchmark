# Why was this SEPA Direct Debit refunded?

There are two possible explanations:

* either the creditor company (or institution) has spontaneously refunded you
* or you requested a refund using the **Request a refund** button in your Qonto app

☝️**Good to know:** you can find this button by clicking on the direct debit in question in the **Business account > Outgoing direct debits** tab of your app.
