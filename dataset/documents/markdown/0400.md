What are the additional fees?
-----------------------------

At Qonto, additional fees may apply when you exceed the number of services included in your plan, for non-euro card payments (depending on the card you have), or for additional services.

* To find out which actions are included in your monthly plan, click **[here](https://qonto.com/en/pricing)**.
* And if you're curious about how these additional fees are calculated, you can read all about it in **[this article](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947674128913)**.

☝️Good to know, certain special operations are now part of your monthly plan! By special operations, we mean:

* Confirmation letters
* Handling non-sufficient funds or rejected checks
* Managing irregularities
* Returning funds due to an issue with a foreign currency transfer

We're here to make your financial experience as smooth as possible!

How can I access the details of my current subscription and additional fees?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

You can get additional information about all Qonto fees by simply clicking on a transaction in your history.

To see the details of your charges for the current month, just head over to **Settings** **> Billing and invoices**



You can find more information about Qonto’s additional fees in your application by simply clicking on the concerned transaction in your history.

👉 Check out [this article](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947671822481) for a better understanding of how billing works.
