# What is the Insurance section and how does it work?

The **Insurance** section on Qonto allows you to explore and subscribe to a wide range of insurance products tailored to your business needs. This platform features offerings from two partner insurers: **Stello 🇫🇷** and very soon with **SureIn 🇩🇪** . You will find an insurance marketplace, an embedded subscription flow to not waste any time and a centralized repository for managing insurance contracts and documents.

Am I eligible?
--------------

All Qonto accounts in France and Germany can access the Insurance section, regardless of their pricing plan, if their activity falls under one of the following categories:

* Digital
* Construction
* Hospitality/Retail
* Ride-hailing/Taxi
* Regulated professions

However, only account Owners and Admins have the ability to use it on the web app.

What kind of insurance products are available in the Insurance section?
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

We offer a variety of insurance products tailored to different industries.

* In France, 12 insurance products are available, including professional liability, legal protection, and insurance for computer equipment.
* In Germany, the insurance product will be launched soon. Stay tuned!

The products shown are filtered based on your company’s activity to ensure relevance.

Who are the insurance providers we partner with?
------------------------------------------------

We partner with well-known insurers such as **Stello** and **SureIn**, very soon. Each insurance product may be offered by different providers, including AXA and AIG, with various plans available for each provider.

How do I apply for a professional liability insurance?
------------------------------------------------------

To apply for a professional liability insurance through Qonto, go to the new **Insurance** section in your account. From there, you can find all the insurance products relevant to your business.

By clicking on a product, you will access more details and have the option to click **Get a Quote**. You will then be redirected to the insurer’s platform (e.g., Stello) to complete your application or be contacted by an agent, depending on the product you choose.

Why should I apply through Qonto and not directly on the partner website?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Applying through Qonto offers a seamless, integrated experience, allowing you to manage all your insurance contracts directly from your Qonto account. Additionally, we ensure that the subscription process meets our quality standards, guaranteeing an optimal user experience.
