# How does Qonto partner with Wise to enhance international transfers?

As the leading European business finance solution for SMEs and freelancers, Qonto is committed to offer the best business banking solution to its customers. To further enhance the payment capabilities offered to its users, **Qonto is now partnering with Wise for international transfers.**

What is Wise?
-------------

Wise is a global technology company, building the best way to move and manage the world’s money. Over the last decade, Wise has built an entirely new network for the world’s money.

By partnering with Wise, companies such as Qonto can leverage Wise’s technology and embed the best way to send, receive, and manage money internationally into their existing infrastructure.

How does the partnership between Wise and Qonto work?
-----------------------------------------------------

This partnership with Wise allows Qonto to **integrate Wise's international payment services**, enhancing the service Qonto provides to its customers.

As a Qonto customer, you will be able to pay your partners faster, in more countries, and with a more transparent and reliable platform.

The key benefits of this partnership are:

* **Global reach:** Qonto allows you to make transfers to over 130 countries, in more than 20 currencies. To know more about this, check out [the list of currencies and countries](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/4359568-how-can-i-submit-swift-transfers#h_4609eda558) [→ link to currency list]
* **Transparency and visibility**: Comprehensive details about fees and the transfer process will be provided before you initiate your transfer. We will also be able to estimate the date of reception of the transfer for your beneficiary.
* **Fast payments:** You can expect fast turnaround times with some transfers settled within one day or even them instantly.
