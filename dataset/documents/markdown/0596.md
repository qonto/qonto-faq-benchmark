# How do I use the Sage x Qonto integration?

This integration is available from [the Account Payable add-on.](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28479048690705)

Sage is an accounting tool for businesses and their professional accountants.

Take advantage of the integration between Qonto and Sage 50c and Sage Business Cloud Accounting on the Connect platform, and synchronize your Qonto transaction information with Sage automatically and securely.

To configure a connection between Qonto and Sage 100, Sage 1000 or Sage X3, please refer to the article ***How do I connect Qonto to my accounting or treasury tool via EBICS?***

**How does the integration work?**

To take advantage of the integration, you will need a Sage account. If you already have an account, go to the Sage web application.

1. *For Sage 50c users:* Log in to your account and open the **Folder** menu, then click **Connected services**. *If you are a Sage Business Cloud Accounting user:* Log in to your account and click **Cash Flow**, then click **Connect Bank** at the bottom right of the bank account you would like to connect.
2. In the **Bankin’** sidebar on the page displayed, click on **Learn more**.
3. On the page introducing the service, click on the **Connect** button.
4. Then select **Qonto**.
5. Enter the **username** and **password** for your bank, and add your account.

You will then be able to set up a bank feed and bank reconciliation.

☝️ **Keep in mind**: The connection between Sage and Qonto is made possible thanks to the Bankin’ aggregator, a completely secure solution to send your Qonto information:

* The connection is protected by your Qonto login information
* Data are encrypted, which means no one can access your codes and data
* The connection complies with the EU directive on PSD2 payments, for optimum protection.
