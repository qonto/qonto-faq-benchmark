# What are the different roles available?

Each member's access is defined by a **role**. A role sets the limits of possible actions on a Qonto account.

### Owner

This role can access all sections and perform any action. They have a full access and they can request the account closure.

### Admin

This role is granted the same rights as the Owner. However, they are not allowed to request the account closure.

### Manager

The manager role is divided into two levels of permissions :

* **Basic Manager :** available with [Business and Enterprise plans](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?select-toggle=custom&periodicity=yearly).
* **Advanced Manager :** available through [the add-ons Account Payable and Expense & Spend Management](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/28479048690705).

Both roles are customizable, allowing users to access only the features designated by then Admins or the Owner. Their scope of permissions apply either on a **Team** level or at the **Whole company** level.

**Note:** All reviews and approval permissions are part of the **Advanced Manager** role only.

You will find the details about the manager role [here](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947656192145).

Both Basic and Advanced Manager roles have access to the [Client Invoices](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947647770897) feature.

### Employee

The Employee role access is limited to their own transactions, receipts, and uploaded invoices. If assigned, they can hold a card and make payments with it.

Additionally, with the [Business and Enterprise plans](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?select-toggle=custom&periodicity=yearly), they can submit transfer requests.

### Accountant

This role is available [from the Smart plan](https://qonto.com/en/pricing?select-toggle=solo). It has been designed to suit the needs of your external accountant, finance advisor, manager, or CPA. With Business and Enterprise plans, they are also able to make transfer requests.
