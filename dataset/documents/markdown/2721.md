# How to activate e-invoicing on Qonto?

***🇮🇹 This article only concerns companies registered in Italy.***

Before starting to use e-invoicing on Qonto, you’ll need to do a quick one-time setup that will connect your Qonto account to the *Sistema di Interscambio*, as required by Italian regulation. This setup process can be completed in less than one minute, only needs be done once per organization, and will allow you to manage all your e-invoicing directly on Qonto.

1. From the navigation menu, click on **Invoicing.**
2. Because you haven’t created any e-invoices yet, this section will appear empty. Click on the black **Get started** button on the center of the screen, which will take you to the activation screen.

On the activation screen, please fill in the following details:

* VAT number
* *Codice fiscale* (if you provided it to us when you first opened your Qonto account, we’ll pre-fill it for you)
* Tax regime
* Province

Finally, please tick the checkbox at the bottom to confirm that you agree with e-invoicing terms and conditions and click on **Continue**.

➡️ You can also receive supplier invoices on Qonto! To do so, add Qonto’s *Codice Destinatario* **(9SUB64Q)** to your account on the *Agenzia delle Entrate’s* website, or share it with your supplier.Supplier invoices that you receive will then appear on the section **Bookkeeping > Supplier invoices** from the navigation menu (for more information, read our [dedicated article](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950969131153)).

That’s it – you’re all set! 🎉 You’ll be automatically redirected to the e-invoice creation screen, where you’ll be able to [issue your first e-invoice](https://help.qonto.com/en/articles/6340727-how-to-create-an-e-invoice) in just a few clicks.
