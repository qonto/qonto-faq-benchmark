# My residence permit has expired, what document do I need to open an account?

If your residence permit has expired, don't worry, we offer a two-step solution to help you open an account with Qonto. Here’s what you need to do:

**Step 1: Verify your identity with your passport:**

Complete the identity verification process via our partner Fourthline using your valid passport as the primary document.

❗️ To maximize the chances of your passport being accepted, ensure you present an original document in good condition, with information that is clearly legible.

💡 [How to take a good picture of your identity card / passport / residence permit?](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947674641553)

**Step 2: Send us a photo of your receipt:**

After completing the identity verification with your passport, send us a photo of your receipt along with your expired residence permit to the following address: [onboarding@qonto.com](mailto:onboarding@qonto.com).

### **⚠️** Accepted types of receipts:

We accept the following receipts as proof of your legal status in France, pending the renewal of your residence permit:

**✅ Receipt of residence permit renewal application**
**✅ Confirmation of favorable decision for the renewal of residence permit application**

ℹ️ **Special cases:** Certificates of favorable decision on first-time residence permit applications are not currently accepted in our process.

By following these steps, you can quickly open your account despite the expiration of your residence permit.

If you have any questions or encounter any difficulties, feel free to contact us at [support@qonto.com](mailto:support@qonto.com), we will be happy to assist you.
