# My card transaction is rejected, what can I do ?

Physical payments, online payments, cash withdrawals.

Here is some common troubleshooting in order to solve a card transaction problem by yourself. First depending on the **payment method**, then on general **card usage and setting**.

Physical **contactless** payment rejected
=========================================

👉 Check that your **[Contactless payments option](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947686243217)** is well activated for this card\*.

👉 Check that you haven’t reached your **limits of €150 consecutive contactless payments**. In which case, it will be necessary to perform a transaction using your PIN code.

👉 Try to **perform the same payment, inserting your card** (PIN code).

Physical **magstripe** payment rejected
=======================================

👉 Check with the merchant that they **allow this payment type**. Indeed, some merchants don't allow it.

👉 Try to **perform the same payment, inserting your card** (PIN code).

Physical **PIN code** payment rejected
======================================

👉 Check with the merchant that the **payment terminal doesn't have an Internet connection problem**, which can cause a payment rejection.

**Online** payment rejected
===========================

👉 Check that the **card information** have been filled in well :

1. Are the **expiration date and the CVV number** correct?
2. Is the card being indicated as a **debit card** and not a credit card?
3. Are the **embossed name and billing/shipment addresses** correct?
4. Is the **name registered on the site** the same as your company's?

👉 Check that the **phone number** registered for a **[3DS authentication](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947671680529)** matches yours.

👉 Check that you haven't activated an **ad blocker or a plugin** that could prevent the payment from being performed.

**Payment in installments**
===========================

Please note that the possibility of paying in installments depends on the **merchant**.

The merchant can choose one of the following options:
​

* **Refuse debit cards**, as there is no guarantee that the funds will be available at the next deadline
* **Blocking the full amount** (up to 8 days)
* **Blocking a partial amount** (one third or one quarter)

As Qonto is currently a payment institution, the accounts cannot be in debit (no overdraft allowed). In order to limit the risk that this represents for the merchant, the merchant checks that there are sufficient funds on the account by making an authorization.

Payment in installments is therefore not very compatible with the type of cards we use. If payment in installments do not work, Qonto has no means of action.

**Common troubleshooting for a rejected card transaction**
==========================================================

Card activation
---------------

👉 Check that the card has been **[activated](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947700727569)** *(Physical cards only)*.

Payment limits
--------------

👉 Check that your **account balance** isn't insufficient ;

👉 Check that the **[limits of the card](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947716404369)** aren't exceeded\* ;

👉 Check that you aren't in a **[balance check](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947701967505)** case (automated gas station, hotel, car rental, etc.).

Card blocking
-------------

👉 Check that the card hasn't been **[automatically blocked](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947686148497)**, following 3 wrong PIN codes in a row *(Physical cards only)*.

👉 Check that the card hasn't been **[temporarily blocked](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947700453009)** either by the account Owner, or an Admin, or a Manager\*.

👉 Check that the card hasn't been **[deactivated/deleted](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947700453009)**, after having been declared as lost or stolen.

Use abroad
----------

👉 Check that the **[Payments abroad option](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947686243217)** of your card is well activated\* ;

👉 Check that the country in which you've used the card is not on the **[sensitive countries list](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947693926929)**. In that case, please get in touch with our customer support.

Cash withdrawals
----------------

👉 In the case of a cash withdrawal at an ATM, please keep in mind that it might be sometimes refused by the ATM for one of those two reasons :

- The ATM own limits might block transactions of a certain amount ;

- The ATM might not be detaining enough cash to handle your request.

💬 If you can't find a solution to your problem even with these proposals, **contact us directly by chat from your Qonto interface**, by clicking on the bubble in the bottom right corner. We'll be glad to help you !

*\*If you need to raise your limits, remove a temporary block, or modify your settings, please get in touch either with the account Owner, or an Admin, or your Manager.*
