# How can I order my Qonto card ?

Cards ordering, delivery and activation

With your Qonto account, you can get [different types of cards](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950907046929).

*Please note that only account holders, admins, and managers can perform this action.*

From the section ‘**Business Account**’, click on **'Cards'** and **‘Create Card’** located in the top right corner :

* Choose the **type of card** that fits your needs
* Name the (future) **cardholder**
* [Set the **parameters** of your card](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951037603985)
* Set your **payment** and **withdrawals limits**
* Set **days of use** and **spending categories** (optional)
* Choose your **delivery address** (for physical cards). It will be selected by the person in charge of creating the card. Cards can be delivered to :

  + The cardholder's personal address
  + The company's headquarters
  + A personalized address

* 🚨 Set the PIN code that will be the functional PIN for the use of your card. You can [view your PIN](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951001855249) at anytime.
* Confirm your order via your Qonto app or with the confirmation code sent by sms
* Your card has been ordered 🎉

**Please note:**All cards offered by Qonto are Debit Mastercards. Credit cards are currently not available at Qonto.

☝️ Good to know: For security reasons, a card order is finalized only when its owner has defined the PIN code. For more information on how to set your PIN code, please read the dedicated article : [How do I set the PIN code of my Qonto card?](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951006686097)

Click on this link to view the demo in your own interface: [https://app.qonto.com?tdm=create-new-card](https://app.qonto.com/?tdm=create-new-card)

No need to wait in order to use your card ! They are active right after their creation for online payments and physical ones through Apple Pay or Google Pay before receiving them physically.
