# What should I do if my Qonto card is lost or stolen?

**How can I permanently block a card?**
---------------------------------------

Whether your Qonto card is lost, stolen or even broken, you can **deactivate** it directly from your account:

1. From the section **Business Account**, click on **Cards;**
2. Select the appropriate card and click on **Report lost/stolen**, at the bottom of the right-hand side;
3. Then, select **Report as lost** or **Report as stolen;**
4. Finally, enter your password to confirm the action.

   * If you wish to be guided on your desktop app, please click here for lost: [https://app.qonto.com?tdm=report-lost-card](https://app.qonto.com/?tdm=report-lost-card)
   * If you wish to be guided on your desktop app, please click here for stolen: [https://app.qonto.com?tdm=report-stolen-card](https://app.qonto.com/?tdm=report-stolen-card)

**☝️** **This is an irreversible action that will permanently block your card**.

👉 You can order a new card directly from your interface:

* Select the card you reported as lost / stolen
* Click on **Order a new card**

**What should I do if there is a fraudulent operation?**
--------------------------------------------------------

You just checked your last transactions, and you think you are being defrauded? We explain to you what to do, step by step, [right here](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949901844497).

If you have any doubts about a transaction, you can start a chat with us by logging in your Qonto interface. We would be happy to help you.
