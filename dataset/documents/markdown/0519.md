# Who is our insurance partner, Stello?

**Stello** is a digital insurance marketplace that specializes in providing tailored insurance solutions for entrepreneurs, freelancers, and small businesses. It offers a wide range of insurance products that are customized based on the specific needs and activities of each customer.

Who can use Stello?
-------------------

Whether you run a digital startup, a construction firm, or a retail shop, Stello has insurance plans that cater to various business activities and industries.

What types of insurance products are available through Stello?
--------------------------------------------------------------

Stello offers a broad spectrum of insurance products, including:

* **Professional Liability Insurance**: Protects against claims of negligence or mistakes in professional services.
* **Equipment Insurance**: Covers damages or losses to business equipment, such as computers or machinery.
* **Legal Protection Insurance**: Provides coverage for legal disputes related to your business operations.

The specific products available to you will depend on your business's industry and unique needs.

How do I subscribe to an insurance product via Stello?
------------------------------------------------------

When you select an insurance product through the Qonto Insurance Hub (**Insurance** > **Professional Liability Insurance** > **Get a Quote**), you will be redirected to the Stello platform.

There, you will be asked to provide your consent and can either immediately choose an insurance plan that’s been tailored to your business, or you may be contacted by a Stello agent for more personalized assistance. The exact process may vary depending on the insurance product you choose.

**Note:** once you signed your contract, it will be accessible from Stello platform. If you wish to access it from Qonto, go to the Stello customer area and click on "Log in with Qonto". Once you've completed the authentification, your contract will be displayed in your Qonto app.

What makes Stello different from other insurance providers?
-----------------------------------------------------------

Stello stands out by offering a highly personalized insurance experience. Instead of providing generic insurance plans, Stello customizes its offerings based on the specific industry, size, and needs of your business. This ensures that you get the most relevant coverage without paying for unnecessary extras.

What information does Stello need to provide a customized insurance plan?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

To offer a tailored insurance solution, Stello will typically ask for details about your business activity, company size, and specific risks associated with your industry. This information helps them match you with the most appropriate insurance products and plans.
