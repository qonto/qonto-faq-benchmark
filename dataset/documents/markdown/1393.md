# Where can I find the contract I signed when I registered?

Right after your registration, an email called **'Welcome to Qonto'** is sent to you.

The commitment between Qonto and the customer is now materialized **by the acceptance of our TOS (Terms and Conditions).**

When registering, the person who opens the account **accepts our TOS by checking the acceptance field.** ✅

💡If you don't find this email :

* please have a look at your spam mailing box
* check this following section on our website : [Legal documents](https://support-de.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949178941329)
