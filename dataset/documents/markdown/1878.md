# My account has been suspended due to outdated information, what should I do?

We've recently updated our policies and, like all payment institutions, we require some additional information from our clients. This process will take just a few minutes of your time.

To update your information, please follow these steps:

1. Go to ⚙️ **Settings** > **Organization details**
2. Click on the **pencil icon** next to your account name and **edit your business information**.
3. **Save the changes** and don’t forget to click on **Submit changes** to complete the update.

**What happens if I don’t submit the update?**

If you do not complete the required update, your account will remain suspended. It is important that you proceed to avoid any interruption in service.
