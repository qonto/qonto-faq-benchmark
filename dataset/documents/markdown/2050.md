# How does the X-Card promotional offer work?

Qonto provides physical and virtual Mastercard debit cards (you can have [up to 5 of each if desired](https://qonto9015-it.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23950014229521)). These cards must be attached to a real user of Qonto and can be configured to meet their specific needs. Online payments, contactless payments or payments with PIN code entry.

We are **launching a promotional offer on X-card this month, 3rd of July 2023** : we are offering the **X-card for free for 3 months.**

**How can I enjoy the special offer and get a metal X Card free for 3 months?**

The metal X Card is free for 3 months for each new customer who opens a Qonto account (whichever plan you choose).

To take advantage of this offer, follow these steps:

1. Copy the offer code: **XCARD3M**
2. [Start the account opening process](https://welcome.qonto.com/signup/select-country) on [Qonto.com](http://Qonto.com)
3. When prompted to select your plan, click on **Do you have a promo code?** in the top right-hand corner.



**4. Enter the discount code: XCARD3M** and then confirm.

*NB: it will be written that the promo code has been applied*

5. Continue the register process

6. After your contract is signed, **order your Metal X Card**



7. You’re done! You can now **enjoy your Metal X Card for free for the next 3 months**.

At the end of your free 3 months, you will automatically be billed for your X Card at the standard fee of €20 per month excluding tax.

*NB: the voucher is active immediately.*

👆 This means that if you use it on 17/07/2023, you will not pay this X card from 17/07/2023 until 16/10/2023, you will then pay pro rata in October between 17/10/2023 and 31/10/2023.
