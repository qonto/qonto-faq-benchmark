# Why was my card blocked?

**You have blocked your PIN**
-----------------------------

If you have entered 3 wrong PIN when withdrawing or when performing a physical payment, the status of your card becomes “**PIN blocked**”.

You will need to follow the process to **[change your PIN here](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947686148497).**

☝️ Upon receipt of your card, if you do not remember **the first PIN defined during the order**, it won't be possible, for security reasons, to request a reset.

In this case, you will have to [delete this card](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947680825233) and [order a new one](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947709197329).

**Your card is temporarily blocked (paused)**
---------------------------------------------

It is possible to temporarily block your card and pause it directly from your customer space.

**How can I unblock a card following a temporarily blocking ?** 🔓

* From the section **‘Business Account’,** click on **'Cards'**
* Select the card you want to unsuspend
* Click on **'Unblock card'**
* Type in your password and select **'Unblock this card'**
* Submit your choice.

If you are an employee, you will need the validation of your Admin or Manager.
