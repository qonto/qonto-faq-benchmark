# I have the documents signed by the notary. How can I proceed with the opening of the business account for my company?

After finalizing the signing of the articles of incorporation and the company bylaws with the notary, you only need to send us a signed and stamped copy of these documents to our email address, **[iscrizione@qonto.com](mailto:iscrizione@qonto.com)**.

Our team will perform a review of the documentation, and you will receive a response within the next 48 hours. **[What verifications does Qonto perform and why?](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949976546833)**

Has the notary not yet provided you with a copy of the deed of incorporation?
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the event that the notary needs to submit the original copy of the deed of incorporation to the Chamber of Commerce to finalize the creation of your company, we can also accept a **notarized certification signed and stamped by the notary**, clearly indicating:

* Date of the company's establishment;
* Name of the newly established company;
* Company address;
* The entire purpose of the company;
* Name, surname, and codice fiscale of all partners (for individuals);
* Name, REA (Repertorio Economico Amministrativo), and codice fiscale (for legal entities);
* Percentage breakdown of corporate shares;
* Method of payment of the share capital;
* Name, surname, and codice fiscale of the administrator.

**👉 By following [this link](https://qonto-assets.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/intercom_faq/Template+-+Certificato+notarile.pdf), you can see an example of an accepted notarial certificate.**

Are there any other partners in your company besides you?
---------------------------------------------------------

If there are other partners in your company besides you, please make sure to attach a photograph of the identity documents of the beneficial owners of your company to your email. **[Why does Qonto need to identify the beneficial owners of my company?](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949967058577)**

💡 Ensure that the photographs of your partners' documents meet our security criteria: **[How to take a good photo of your ID card and driver's license](https://support-it.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23949941631121)**
