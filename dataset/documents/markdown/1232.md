# How can I submit SWIFT transfers in euros?

You can make **a EUR transfer** through our partner Wise which enables you to process it using the SWIFT network.

You will be able to **send EUR to any country from the list** **below** **with the SWIFT network**.

You can make a **SWIFT transfer in EUR** with the same level of speed and easiness as with Wise, directly from your Qonto account. You do not need a Wise account.

**What is the fee?**
====================

The fees for SWIFT transfers in EUR applied are of €5/transfer.

👆**Good to know:** the funds will be sent using the SHA method (other intermediary banks may also charge fees).

**What's the receiving time? ⏱**

You will be able to submit SWIFT transfers in EUR at anytime.

It is necessary to consider the bank holidays of the banks through which the transfer will pass.

Different time zones can also affect the delay of transmission and reception of the transfer.

👆**Good to know:**

SWIFT transfers in EUR can be executed during markets open hours only.

This means that most of banks don’t process transfers during weekends and bank holidays. In a case of SWIFT transfers in EUR, please keep in mind that you have several holidays around the world, where banks are not working. Your transfers will be processed the next open day during markets open hours for most of the currencies.

At this day, your Qonto account does not allow you to transfer currencies to individuals that are not businesses but professional such as **contractors/freelancers** are allowed.

In case you want to pay an individual, please provide a contractual agreement that highlights the payment purpose.

Your transfer might be declined if supporting documentation is not provided.
