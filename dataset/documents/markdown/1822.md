# How to close my Qonto account?

🖥️ You can request the closure of your account by following the steps below **from a computer**:

1. Go to **Settings > Organization details**.
2. Select the **Close Account** button.
3. Follow the guidance until you reach the confirmation page.

Use this demo link for more details: <https://app.qonto.com/?tdm=close-my-account>

💡Upon initiating the closure of your account, you will shortly receive a proportional refund for the unused portion of your subscription fee, card subscription fees and E&SM add-on subscription fee covering the period from the closing date to the end of your current billing cycle.

Please note that the account closure option is **not available from the Qonto mobile app.**

**When can I close my account?**
================================

You have the right to close your account at any time, without any conditions.

**What do I need to ensure before closing my account?**
=======================================================

Before closing your Qonto account, it's important to ensure a few key aspects:

* You are the account holder. Only the account holder has the right to request its closure.
* No collective proceedings (judicial reorganization, judicial liquidation, deregistration, etc.) are in progress. The withdrawal is subjected to the conclusion of these procedures.
* **Your account balance is €0**, and there are no pending transactions (transfers, card payments, check deposits).

**Frequently Asked Questions**
==============================

**When will the deactivation of my account occur once the right of termination has been exercised?**
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The permanent deactivation of your account will occur the next following day.

You can track progress of your account closing request in your web app (Settings account > Price Plan & Invoices).

**Can I reopen my account if it has been closed?**
--------------------------------------------------

No, a closed account is permanently deactivated and cannot be reactivated in the future. Its closure results in the **immediate, permanent, and irrevocable** deactivation of your IBAN(s), cards, and the inability to receive or send transfers.

*NB: You do not need to return your payment cards.*

However, you can cancel your account closure **before the deactivation date** by going to **Settings > Cancel Closure >** Billing & invoices, and select Cancel closure. This option is **available only on the web app.**

**Does the closure incur any fees?**
------------------------------------

No, closing your Qonto account is free.

**Do I still have access to my data after closing my account?**
---------------------------------------------------------------

Yes, even if your account is closed, Qonto provides you with **unlimited access to access** your transaction history and account statements.
