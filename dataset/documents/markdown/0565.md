# How can I add money to my account for the first time?

After creating your Qonto account, you have four different options for topping it up for the first time. They are available on all plans.

1. **Top up by card**

   [You can top up your Qonto account](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947665588881) using a different bank card of yours (Visa or Mastercard only) or via Google/Apple Pay. The funds will appear on your account instantly. The minimum amount is 50 € and the maximum is 2 000 €. It is currently available on desktop only and it can be used only once per organisation.

   Your card details will be retained if you fail to pay your first Qonto subscription fee.
   ​

   💡 Only the holder will have access to the payment method and can delete it by going to Settings > Pricing & Billing > Payment Methods.
2. **Initiate an instant SEPA-in**

   In case your current banks allows you to emit Instant SEPA transfers, [you can top up your Qonto account by transfer in 10 seconds](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947739917969), any day of the week.
3. **Initiate a standard SEPA-in**

   [Standard SEPA transfers](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947739917969) also work. It can take up to 2 to 3 days.
4. **Receive a payment**

   You can [download and share your IBAN details](https://support-fr.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23947723654673) with a third party for instance with your customers and ask them to make their next payment into your Qonto account.

☝️ **Good to know**: You can add money to your account right after your contract has been signed. It will be available for you to use (e.g send money out of your account) once your account has been validated, it usually takes up to 5 to 6 hours from the account opening.
