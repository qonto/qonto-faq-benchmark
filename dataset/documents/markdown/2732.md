# What is my phone number used for?

Your phone number is required in **two cases**:

* It allows us to contact you quickly if we notice suspicious activity on your account.
* It enables you to use the [3-D Secure authentication](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951020664593), which secures online purchases.

For these reasons, it is very important that you make sure that your contact details are **always up to date** in your user profile ([see how to update your information](https://qonto9015-es.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951000065937)).
