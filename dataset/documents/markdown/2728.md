# Why was my transfer declined?

If your transfer was declined, we recommend to follow these steps:

* Please check if there's **enough money** in your balance to process this transfer
* Make sure that you select the right operation type (single euro transfer or single non-euro transfer)
* Make sure that **the name of the beneficiary you have registered matches exactly the one indicated on the RIB**
* For regulatory reasons, we may not have been able to complete the transfer

Do not hesitate to [contact](https://support-es.qonto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23951055377041) our Customer Support Team for more information.
