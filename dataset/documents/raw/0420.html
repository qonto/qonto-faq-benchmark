Do you know other businesses that could use a boost? Introduce them to Qonto and earn a reward!

 

🇮🇹 🇪🇸 Italian and Spanish companies: for each successful referral, both you and your referee can earn €80.

🇩🇪 German companies: for each successful referral, you can earn €150 and your referee €100.

🇫🇷 French companies: for each successful referral, both you and your referee can earn up to €160 each.

 

Who is eligible for the referral offer?

All customers with an active account can refer other companies to Qonto. The referred company must not already have an account with Qonto.

☝️ Important: For a successful referral, it is mandatory that the new client register through the referral link. If the new account was created directly on the Qonto website, no reward will be applicable.

⚠️ As stated in our Terms and Conditions, Qonto may decide at any moment to cancel the distribution of the reward in case notably of abuse of the program and/or breach of our terms of use.

 

How do I share my referral link?

🖥️ From the web app

  1. Log into your Qonto account, click on the small gear icon in the bottom left and select Recommend Qonto.
  2. From the page that opens, you can share your referral link:
    1. Manually: click on “copy referral link” and paste it where you want to share it.
    2. Automatically: add your contacts’ e-mail addresses under “send an invitation via e-mail”. Then press “send” to generate a preset message to refer them to Qonto.

📲 From the mobile app

  1. From your iOS or Android mobile app, go to Menu > Recommend Qonto
  2. Finally, click on “Share link” to send your referral link over through your favourite social media, messages, e-mail or just copy-paste the link manually.

 

How do I collect my referral reward?

Depending on where your business is based, the collect of the referral reward works differently.

☝️Important: Referral links are not taken into account by the system if they are opened in private browsing mode.

💡 The amount of the reward that will be credited to your account will always be the amount effective at the time of the registration of the referee (not necessarily the amount that was effective at the time the referral was made).

 

🇫🇷 If you have been referred to Qonto and have a French company:

  • Open your Qonto account through the referral link shared by your referrer,

  • Order a physical card

  • To receive your initial €80: use your physical card to spend a total of at least €50 (either in a single transaction or multiple transactions, in-store or online) within the first 60 days after your account validation date.

  • To receive the second reward of €80: use your card to spend a total of €3000 (either in a single transaction or multiple transactions, in-store or online) within the first 60 days from the account validation date.

  • That’s all! Your reward will be automatically credited to your account within a maximum of 7 (business) days following the validation of your referral.

    Excluding temporary special offers, the referral reward amount is set at a maximum of €160 for both the referrer and the referee.

    For more details about the referral process, please refer to the dedicated terms and conditions document for FR companies.

 

🇩🇪 🇮🇹 🇪🇸 If you have been referred to Qonto and have a German, Italian or Spanish companies:

  • Open your Qonto account through the referral link that was shared with you

  • Make sure you order a physical card

  • Make a settled transaction with your physical card for an amount of 50€ or more (you can make either one or several transactions, in store or online);

  • That’s all! Your reward will be automatically credited to your account within a maximum of 7 (business) days following the validation of your referral.

    For more details about the referral process, please refer to the dedicated terms and conditions document for DE, ES and IT companies.

 

What transactions are not eligible?

Not all card transactions are eligible for receiving the reward. For instance, the following card transactions are excluded from the referral program:

  • Cash withdrawals,
  • All card transactions that involve the transfer of funds (e.g. gift card purchases, money orders, prepaid card purchases, cryptocurrency purchases, traveller's checks, bank accounts top-up),
  • All card transactions in restaurants, liquor stores or tobacco shops,
  • Betting, casinos and other games,
  • Online payment services provider,
  • Clothing stores.

 

If you have referred someone to Qonto:

Your reward will be automatically credited to your account within a maximum of 7 (business) days after the company you referred completes the steps described above.

💡 Good to know: When a company signs up to Qonto through your referral link, you will be able to track the referral status on the Qonto Web app or on the iOS or Android apps:

  • Web app: once your referee signs up, go to the referral page and you will see a right-hand sidebar where their referral status can be tracked.

  • Mobile app: once your referee signs up, go to the referral page and click on “Reward status” section to see the status of your referral. You will see a button, “Track your referrals”. You can click on that button to view the status of your referral.
