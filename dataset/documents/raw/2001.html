Opening a VAT number is often perceived as a complex procedure that generates many uncertainties. To simplify it, Qonto has partnered with Fiscozen, which offers exclusive benefits to its clients. Let's discover how it works together.
 

What is Fiscozen?

Fiscozen is a fully-fledged digital tax assistant: it is an online platform aimed at making tax management simpler and more accessible for everyone, especially freelancers and small businesses. With the support of expert consultants, Fiscozen helps you open your VAT number in 24 hours and efficiently manage crucial aspects such as electronic invoicing and accounting.
 

Exclusive benefits of the partnership between Qonto and Fiscozen

The collaboration between Qonto and Fiscozen brings a series of significant advantages for those wishing to start their business with a VAT number:
  • €50 discount on the Fiscozen service: if you are a Qonto customer, you can open a VAT number with Fiscozen and benefit from an immediate €50 discount on the services offered by our partner.
  • Free months on Qonto plans: if you are a Fiscozen customer, you are entitled to a discount code to enjoy 4 months free on the Basic plan, or 2 months free on the Smart and Premium plans. This way, you can explore Qonto's features and further optimize the financial management of your business.

 

How to take advantage of these benefits

To access the offer, you simply need to:
  • Visit the web page dedicated to the partnership between Qonto and Fiscozen
  • Click on Open your VAT number
  • Fill out the form with your details and confirm
  • A Fiscozen consultant will contact you as soon as possible to easily start the opening of your VAT number.
The discount on Fiscozen services is applied automatically, and once the VAT number is opened, you will have the discount code to benefit from the free months on Qonto plans.
