We have introduced a new Account Receivable (AR) add-on for advanced invoicing features, while maintaining our free standard invoicing solution.

If you have the AR add-on activated on your account, this article explains:

  • The reasons why you have it activated
  • What's changing after April 1st
  • How to manage your subscription if you prefer standard invoicing only
  • What are the features included in the standard invoicing tool and in the AR add-on

 

Why do I have the Accounts Receivable Add-On activated on my account?

Starting October 1st, 2024, the AR add-on was offered as a free trial.

  1. You may have subscribed to the free trial by yourself
  2. You were using recurring invoices or automated payment reminders before the implementation of the AR add-on, so we automatically activated it to your account to ensure continuity in your workflow

What are the pricing changes from April 1st?

Starting April 1st, 2025, the Accounts Receivable Add-On becomes a paid service.

Public price :

  • Monthly billing: €45/month excl. VAT
  • Annual billing: €35/month excl. VAT (€420 billed once)

Exclusive promotional offers from April 1st to June 30th:

  1. Monthly Billing Offer : • €35/month excl. VAT in April, May, and June • Automatically applied from April 1st for client already using the AR add-on.
  2. Annual Billing Offer : • €29/month excl. VAT for one year (€348 billed once) • To be activated through your account settings before June 30th

Can I opt out of this add-on?

Yes, you can opt out of the add-on for free before April 1st. After April 1st :

  • If the add-on is billed monthly, you can cancel it anytime.
  • If the add-on is billed annually, it’s a one-year commitment—you can opt out, but no refund will be granted.

You can manage your subscription in your account settings. For detailed instructions, see our article "How to change my Qonto subscription plan or billing frequency?”

 

Good to know: You'll keep access to all standard invoicing features after removing the AR add-on.
