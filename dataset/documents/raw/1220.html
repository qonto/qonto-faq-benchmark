In the questionnaire we sent you to complete the request to update your company data, we asked you: "What will be the main source of income for your company in the future?" and asked you to attach documentation that supports the selection made.

 

💡 To help you send us the right documents, below you will find a list of documents you can send us based on the selection made.

👉 If you need to send us multiple documents, remember to bundle them into a PDF file.

 

Business-to-business (B2B) Product Sales:

  • List of issuers and beneficiaries and/or
  • Sales contracts
  • Invoices and payment receipts
  • Orders

B2B Service Fees:

  • Service contracts
  • Invoices for services provided
  • Activity or project reports

B2B Subscription Fees:

  • Subscription contracts
  • Periodic invoices
  • Records of recurring payments

Royalties:

  • License agreements
  • Royalty records
  • Invoices for granted licenses

Commission:

  • Broker agreements
  • Commission invoices
  • Sales or transaction reports

B2B Partnerships and Cooperations:

  • Partnership agreements
  • Joint venture reports
  • Invoices or documentation of shared profits

Training and Workshops:

  • Training contracts
  • Enrollments
  • Invoices for conducted courses or workshops

B2B Advertising and Sponsorship:

  • Advertising or sponsorship contracts
  • Advertising invoices
  • Advertising campaign reports

B2C Product Sales:

  • Sales receipts and invoices
  • Inventory reports
  • Customer orders

B2C Service Fees:

  • Service contracts
  • Service invoices
  • Customer payment proofs

B2C Subscription Revenue:

  • Subscription contracts
  • Recurring invoices
  • Payment history

B2C Advertising Revenue:

  • Advertising contracts

  • Reports on ad impressions

Affiliate Marketing:

  • Affiliate agreements
  • Commission reports
  • Affiliate invoices

E-Commerce Platforms:

Retail Operation:

Licensing and Merchandising:

Crowdfunding and Pre-orders:
