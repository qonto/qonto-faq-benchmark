To comply with the rules imposed by the Code monétaire et financier, we are required to verify the authenticity of the identity documents of all Qonto users, as well as the information they contain. These checks are necessary to prevent fraud, money laundering and terrorism.

 

We accept different types of documents based on country registration:

  • French companies

  • Italian Companies

  • Spanish companies

  • German companies

For Belgian, Dutch, Portuguese, and Austrian companies, we accept the following identity documents:

  • ID card
  • Passport
  • Residence permit
