Automate the processing of your receipts and streamline the payment of your suppliers

With the Supplier Invoices section, you can manage your supplier invoices conveniently from a single tab, the Inbox:

  1. Collect your invoices

  2. Attach your receipts and paid invoices to the transactions (Learn more).

  3. Easily prioritize overdue invoices, review details, and make payments in just a few clicks.

Once an invoice is matched or its payment is created, Qonto removes it from your Inbox, leaving only the invoices that require your attention!

 

If you have choose your plan before the 01/10/2024, this feature is accessible from the mobile and web app to:

  • Account holders and admins of Smart and higher plans.

  • Managers with Whole Company scope and the Upload and set up supplier invoices permission. They can also make direct payments to suppliers (within transfer limits) if they have the Make and approve transfers permission.

  • Accountants with view-only mode.

If you have changed your plan after the 01/10/2024, this feature is accessible on all plans, via mobile and web apps, for the following roles:

  • Account holders and admin. 
  • Managers with the Whole Company scope and the Upload and set up supplier invoices permission. They can also make direct payments to suppliers (within transfer limits) if they have the Make transfers and reimbursements / Make and approve transfers, and reimburse expenses including mileage (only available with Add-on Accounts Payable and/or Add-on Expense & Spend Management) permission.
  • Accountants, with view-only mode.

 

💡 Note: The Supplier Invoices section is not for processing customer invoices.

It is also possible to setup multi-layer approval workflows to keep control over big amounts to reimburse or pay. Approval workflows are available through Account Payable and Expense & Spend Management Add-Ons. More information about approval workflows here.

 

How does it work?

To access to the Supplier invoices section:

  1. Go to the Invoices section :

    1. From the web app, select the Invoices icon on the navigation menu

    2. From the mobile app, click Plus in the lower right corner

  2. Select the Supplier invoice section

On the top of your screen, you can get insights on the total amount of:

  • Invoices in your inbox

  • Invoices in pending

  • Invoices for which there is a scheduled payment

  • Invoices paid in the last 30 days.

 

Collect invoices:

Collect all your invoices, either paid or unpaid, in your Inbox:

  • Using the Upload invoices button of your Supplier Invoices section, or directly dragging and dropping your documents at the center of the Imported tab, in the web app.

  • From your email box, using your forwarding email address.

  • From Google Drive or Dropbox.

  • By synchronising the invoice in transaction attachments

💡 Note: Invoices and receipts added manually to transactions from your History will not appear in your Supplier invoices section.

 

 

Pay or match invoices:

  1. Already paid invoices:
    • Qonto automatically matches receipts and paid invoices to the right transaction and moves the invoice from the All invoices to the Completed tab of the Supplier Invoices section.
    • If we could not match the invoice to the relevant transaction, go to the supplier invoices section and click on "...” next to the invoice you want to edit. Then on Mark as paid to manually attach the invoice to the relevant transaction.
    • You can also decide not to match the invoice with a transaction, if you don't wish to associate the invoice with any.

    • 💡From the Transactions section you can also search for invoices using the Find on Qonto option.

  2. Unpaid invoices:
  • For the imported invoices in the supplier invoices section, click on the invoice to:
    • Edit the payment details.
    • Pay either with a classic SEPA transfer, an international transfer, a scheduled SEPA transfer, an instant transfer. Eligible customers can also take advantage of Pay Later options to finance their invoice payment.
  • Once the transfer is created, Qonto moves the invoice to Completed in the case of an instant transfer or in the Scheduled payments tab for the other type of transfers. For the latter, the invoice will be moved to Completed once the transfer is executed.
  • Qonto automatically attaches the invoice to the corresponding transaction in your Business Account > Transactions section.
  • You will also be able to download your proof of transfer once it has been executed.

 

Your Supplier invoices area does not allow you to make bulk transfers, to pay by card, or to set up your SEPA direct debits.

Match credit notes to an invoice

  • Qonto automatically matches the credit note to the correct transaction and moves the document from the 'All Invoices' tab to the 'Completed' tab in the Supplier Invoices section if we are able to detect the correct transaction ourselves.
  • If the credit note has not been automatically matched to the relevant transaction, go to the Supplier Invoices section, click on '...' next to the line you want to edit, and then select 'Match Transaction' to manually attach the credit note to the relevant transaction.
  • You can also choose not to match the credit note with a transaction if you don't wish to associate the document with any.

Cancel or reschedule payment:

To cancel a scheduled transfer through the Supplier Invoices section:

  1. Check the transfer status to ensure it hasn't been executed yet. (Learn more about transfer execution dates here).

  2. On the Web app, go to the Business account section on the navigation menu, then navigate to Transactions or Transfers. From the mobile app, go to the History or Transfers section. Select the transfer you want, and click Cancel transfer at the bottom left.

If you initiated the transfer from the Supplier Invoices section and it's been cancelled, declined, or returned, the invoice will return to your Inbox.

 

Delete a credit note

To delete a Credit note, click the three dots "..." on the Credit note or select the invoice and click the trash can icon 🗑️ in the top right corner.

 

Find an invoice:

You can filter invoices by supplier, payment status, and alert status using the drop down menus at the top of the page.

You can also use the search bar to perform a text search by supplier name or invoice number.

Click any of the column headers, such as Supplier, Status, or Due date, to sort the invoices by that column.

 

Find a credit note

In the Supplier Invoices section, click on the 'All Invoices' tab, and then click on the 'Amount' column header at the top-right to bring up the credit notes first.

 

Avoiding Duplicates:

To prevent paying the same supplier invoice twice, a notification will appear during the import of your invoice if our system suspects a duplicate.

 

In this case, you can click on Review Invoices on the notification. A screen will then display the imported invoice along with potential duplicates.

You can then decide whether you want to keep or delete it.

 

Manage your suppliers list

You can easily edit or delete your suppliers from the Supplier list section.

  1. In the Invoices section, under Suppliers, click on Supplier list
  2. Find your supplier and click on it, a column will open on the right
  3. In the top right corner, click the pen to edit or the bin to delete

☝️ Good to know :You can not modify the IBAN from this section. To do so, you have to go to the Transfer section, see How to add, edit or delete a beneficiary (SEPA Transfers beneficiary)?
