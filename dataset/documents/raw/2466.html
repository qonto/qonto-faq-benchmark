For SMEs (Small and Medium Enterprises)

To comply with regulations, you must identify and provide information about:

1. Key People to Identify

  • Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs): Anyone owning more than 25% of the company or exercising effective control.
  • Authorized account operators: People with authority to manage the company’s accounts.
  • Elected representatives or administrators: Individuals officially representing or managing the company.
     

2. Required Information for Each Person

  • Full name
  • ID number (with a copy of the document)
  • Birthdate
  • Nationality
  • Country of residence
  • Role or ownership percentage


For complex ownership structures: You must show the complete chain of ownership leading to the individuals who ultimately control the company.

If none: If a category does not apply, simply state “None.”

 

For Freelancers

1. Who to Identify

  • Authorized persons: Anyone who has the authority to operate your account or control your business on your behalf.
     

2. Required Information

  • Full name
  • ID number
  • Birthdate
  • Nationality
  • Country of residence

If you can't provide certain documents: Please let us know which documents you are missing and why—our team will help you find an alternative solution.

 

For Homeowner’s Associations

1. Who to Identify

  • Property administrator: The individual responsible for managing the association’s operations.
  • Major owners: Anyone holding a participation quota of 25% or more.
  • Elected representatives: Such as the President or Secretary authorized to operate the association’s account.
     

2. Required Information for Each Person

  • Full name
  • ID number
  • Birthdate
  • Nationality
  • Country of residence
  • (For major owners: exact participation percentage)

     

Company Creation

Who are Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) and what information is required?

1. UBOs to Identify

  • Anyone who will own more than 25% of shares or voting rights, or who will have effective control over the company.
     

2. Required Information

  • Full name
  • Copy of ID document
  • Birthdate
  • Nationality
  • Country of residence
  • Exact ownership percentage
     

💡 If formal appointments have not yet been made, include planned or provisional information.

 

 

If you’re ever unsure about what to provide or how to estimate these numbers, our support team is here to help clarify and guide you through the process!
