If you are a freelancer, you can obtain the Certification of allocation of the VAT number associated to your activity in just a click, by logging into the reserved area of the Agenzia delle Entrate online services.

To access it, you can use one of the following methods:

  • SPID (Public Digital Identity System)

  • CIE (Electronic Identity Card)

  • CNS (National Services Card)

Once you have logged in, you will just have to follow the instructions reported here (please note the service is in Italian only).

☝️ Before sending the document, make sure the data reported is up to date.

If you are the legal representative or appointed of your business, you can get the Visura camerale for free through the cassetto digitale dell'imprenditore (please note the service is in Italian only.

To access it, you can choose one of the following methods:

  • SPID (Public Digital Identity System)

  • CNS (National Services Card)

Both are issued for free by the Chamber of Commerce and they certify your identity, by matching it with the role present in the Business Register.

☝️In order to successfully process the updated information of your account, we will require you to send us a Visura camerale downloaded in the last 3 months maximum.
