To open a professional account in Qonto as a freelancer, we accept the following documents:

  1. Form 036/037 or Certificate of Census Situation.
  2. Registration in the Special Regime of Self-Employed Workers of the Social Security or Report of your recent working life (no more than 1 month).

IMPORTANT! Make sure that the CSV code is visible when uploading the document.

 

Additionally, we will need to verify your tax address to comply with our internal policies and applicable regulations. For this, you will need to provide one of the following types of documents:

  • A recent bill (less than 3 months old) for basic utilities such as electricity, water or telephone.
  • A tax document (such as a Census Status Certificate, IBI or tax return)
  • A housing document, which can be a recent rental contract, a rent payment receipt for the last 3 months, or a current home insurance contract/bill.

You must meet these criteria in order to open a Qonto account.
