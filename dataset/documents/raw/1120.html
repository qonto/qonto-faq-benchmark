Requirements for company formation

Company headquarters: Your company is based in Germany
Minimum age: 18 years (exceptions for minors with court approval possible)
Share capital: Minimum 1 Euro for UG, 25,000 Euro for GmbH
ID: For account opening, a valid ID card or passport from the EU/EFTA area, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, United Kingdom, United States or German residence permit. More details about accepted identity documents can be found here.
Documents: We need the commercial register application, the founding document and the list of shareholders. You will receive these documents automatically when founding with beglaubigt.de. Further details about documents can be found here.
Business activity: The company's activity must comply with Qonto's guidelines and must not be among the prohibited activities. The list of activities not permitted at Qonto can be found here.

Timeframe - How long does company formation take?

The duration of the formation process essentially depends on two factors:

  • how quickly you submit your documents and complete your information
  • how quickly the notary appointment and registration by the commercial register take place

Super fast! With Qonto and beglaubigt, you can use your business account in as little as 7-10 business days with an express notary appointment and model protocol.

Below you will find the average processing times for each formation step in detail.

Formation and notary appointment with beglaubigt.de

  • Average duration of notary appointment: on average 3-5 business days (for model protocol formation) to 1.5 weeks (for individual articles of association)
  • Express procedure possible: With complete documents and quick appointment availability, notarization can often take place the following day.

Account opening with Qonto

  • Registration is completed in 5 minutes, identification via video or eID usually takes about 15 minutes. Account processing takes place in real time and parallel to your formation. The account is usually ready for share capital deposit within 48 hours.
  • Save time! The final account activation takes place directly after the share capital deposit based on the notarial commercial register application. No tedious waiting for the final registration or authorities.

Registration in the commercial register

  • Processing at the responsible local court takes an average of 3-7 business days and can vary by region.

Detailed process of company formation with Qonto and beglaubigt

Fill out questionnaire and data collection: • You conveniently enter all relevant master data online - legal form (UG or GmbH), shareholder information, business address, management, company name and business purpose • For multiple shareholders, the information is recorded individually for each person.
 
Automatic contract preparation: • Based on your information, a legally secure articles of association is automatically created • You can choose between model protocol formation (faster) or individual articles of association (customizable) • All documents are provided as ready-to-use templates
 
Appointment confirmation and coordination: • You receive an appointment confirmation by email with all important information • beglaubigt.de coordinates express appointments nationwide and can often offer a certification appointment as early as the following day
 
Notarial certification (digital or in person):
• Digital option: An authorized notary conducts the legally required certification via video conference. Identification is done via eID (online ID function) with the AusweisApp Bund. The notary explains the contract, answers questions and reads the essential contents aloud. At the end, you sign the document electronically – completely legally valid.
 
For more information on applying for an eID, please visit here on the Federal Ministry's website.
 
• In-person option: If digital certification is not desired or possible (no online ID function), an appointment is arranged in person with a notary in your region.
 
Integrated business account opening, optionally with eID •  After the notary appointment, we directly open your Qonto business account – fully integrated into the formation process. Account opening runs parallel to formation and takes 2-4 business days. The eID also enables completely digital identification for account opening for already existing companies via the AusweisApp Bund, alternatively without eID via our partner IDNow using video identification.
 
You immediately receive your account details, even before commercial register entry
 
Share capital deposit: • The share capital is deposited into the already opened Qonto business account • Only after this deposit can the commercial register entry take place • You conveniently send the individual payment proof via Qonto to your notary's email
 
Commercial register entry and authority registrations: • Your personally assigned notary submits all documents to the responsible registry court • In parallel, the business registration, registration with Chamber of Commerce/Chamber of Crafts, tax office (tax number assignment) and transparency register take place
 
Formation completion and account activation• You receive all documents digitally via your personal customer area • Your company is legally formed, fully capable of doing business, with limited liability and the Qonto account ready for use. 
 
Access your account faster. Your Qonto account is immediately ready to use. We only need proof of commercial register application from your notary. The commercial register entry is checked by Qonto in the background, while you can already use your account.
