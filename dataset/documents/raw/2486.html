An instant transfer is a transfer that takes place in a few seconds, unlike a standard transfer that can take up to 3 business days to be received.

And most importantly, this type of transfer works every day, at any time, even on holidays.

 

👆Good to know: One time, scheduled and recurring transfers are configured by default as an instant transfer but you can also choose to uncheck this parameter and make a classic transfer (received within 48 working hours) in the One Last Look step of the flow.

 

For your transfer to be instant, it has to meet two main conditions:

  • t must take place within the SEPA zone (which differs from the Europe zone)

  • it should not exceed 100 000 euros.

 

If you send cash to a supplier on a Saturday, for example, this supplier sees the cash on his account within seconds.

 

At Qonto, sending and receiving instant transfers is free of charge, up to the number of free transfers included in your plan.

 

☝🏻 Please Note: Transfers between Qonto accounts are free of charge, so no additional fees will be charged to your plan for any transfer of this kind.

 

☝🏻 In some cases, the instant transfer option will not be possible, you will need to send it as a standard transfer. 🙏🏻
