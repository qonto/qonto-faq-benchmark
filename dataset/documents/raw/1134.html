  • 🇦🇺 Australia
  • 🇨🇦 Canada

  • 🇳🇿 New Zealand
  • 🇺🇸 United States

Please note that we would need to perform additional checks to verify the eligibility criteria and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements before finalizing the account opening process.

 

The legal representative of the shareholder company is responsible for the payment of the share capital and must be invited to the business account as a shareholder for this purpose.

 

You can upload the extract from the commercial register of the shareholder company/legal entity together with the incorporation documents during the registration.
​

Important: Qonto does not accept shareholder companies that are still in the incorporation phase as shareholders, unless the companies concerned have already opened an account with Qonto.

Additionally, Qonto does not accept shareholder companies whose business purpose is listed in the following Article: What activities are prohibited at Qonto?

 

The legal representative is always indicated on the extract from the commercial register of the legal entity of the shareholder. Any legal form and activity of the legal entity is permitted with the exception of associations.
