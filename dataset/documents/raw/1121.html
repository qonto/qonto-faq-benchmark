beglaubigt.de is Europe's first digital notary and an innovative platform for notarial services that has been revolutionizing the German market since 2022.

Companies and individuals can process notarial procedures such as company formations, powers of attorney, apostilles, or registry changes completely online - securely, quickly, and in compliance with legal requirements. The platform combines modern technology with German notarial tradition while saving valuable time and unnecessary paperwork.

Behind beglaubigt.de is an interdisciplinary team of legal professionals and technology experts dedicated to modernizing notarial processes. 

The company was founded with the goal of digitizing notarial processes without compromising on legal certainty or quality. The headquarters is located in Munich; our partner works exclusively with notaries licensed in Europe.

Legal Basis and Security
The service of beglaubigt.de is based on the Digitalization Directive (DiRUG) of July 5, 2021, which officially came into force on August 1, 2022. Since this renewal, digital certifications have been possible in Germany and Austria, which forms the legal basis.

International Recognition
The digital certifications from are legally recognized in over 113 countries as they strictly follow the Hague Apostille Convention. This makes them ideal for international business activities and visa applications.

Data Protection and Security:
All documents are stored securely on European servers and comply with GDPR. The platform uses SSL encryption and ensures the highest data protection and security standards. Collaboration is exclusively with certified European notaries with years of experience ranging from corporate law to family law.
