Can I make check payments ?

As of today, we do not offer neither checkbooks, or checks.

Therefore, it is not possible to make check payments with your account.

 

Indeed, Qonto is an online payment institution, which offers exclusively dematerialized services.

However, we allow cashing checks on an exceptional basis if your customers have no other means of payment.
