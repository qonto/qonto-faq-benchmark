👉 This article only concerns accounts whose IBAN started with TRZO before the migration to our Qonto banking platform (QNTOFR).

You can access transactions from your historical Treezor account with Qonto’s banking platform.

How do I access my historical Treezor account information?

From your web application:

  1. Log in to your account with your Qonto ID and password.

  2. Go to the Settings section,

  3. Select Historical Data.

In this section, you will be able to retrieve:

  • Your account statements

  • Your invoices

  • An export of your transactions, with or without the associated receipts
