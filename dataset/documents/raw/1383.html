What is a card of domiciliation?

 

A card of domiciliation (or direct debit card) connects the payment terminal to a company's bank account and is especially useful for craftsmans or merchants. It generally has 7 digits and is known as a "retail card for POS". It is required for the configuration of the payment terminal (POS).

 

With a direct debit card, you can:

  • Collect the payment by credit card

  • Refund the transaction

  • Cancel the transaction

 

☝️ As a payment institution and not a banking institution, Qonto does not provide a direct debit card yet.
