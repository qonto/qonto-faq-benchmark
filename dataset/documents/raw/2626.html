Quipu is the invoicing and accounting tool for SMEs and freelancers, which simplifies and automates your financial and accounting management.

 

Connect Qonto with Quipu to manage your invoices and cash flow more easily.

Automate your transactions, income and expenses, thanks to the integration of Qonto and Quipu.

 

 

How to connect Quipu to Qonto?

  1. Log in to your Quipu account;

  2. Click on the Banks section;

  3. Select the + Add bank account button;

  4. Click on the Connect my bank button;

  5. At the bottom, you will find the Show all available banks button. Click on;

  6. Search for Qonto and select;

  7. Here you will be prompted for Qonto login credentials.
    - Go to your Qonto account and click on Settings icon
    - Then select API Key
    - Copy the login credentials (Sign-in and Secret Key)
    - Paste the login credentials (Sign-in and Secret Key) you have previously copied into Qonto.

 

Done! Account connected.
