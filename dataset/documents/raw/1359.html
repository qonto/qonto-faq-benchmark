You can open a Qonto business account if you:


• Are the legal representative of the company, or
• Have a power of attorney and proof of identity of a legal representative.


In both cases, simply follow this link: https://welcome.qonto.com/ to open the account.

Account opening as a legal representative
If you are a legal representative, follow the classic account opening process by clicking on one of the displayed names. If you are a legal representative but do not appear on the list, click on "Enter your information".

Account opening with power of attorney
If you are not a legal representative, you will need a power of attorney and an ID of the legal representative to open an account. Please note that we will request this information during the account opening process.

Important Notice
You can only use your company's Qonto account for business activities, such as paying salaries, paying invoices, etc. Using the Qonto account to collect funds on behalf of third parties is not allowed. In this case, Qonto will close your account with a 30-day notice.
