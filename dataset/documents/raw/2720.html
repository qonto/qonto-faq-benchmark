Here are the different steps you have to follow to add your Qonto card on your phone:

  1. From the section ‘Business Account’, click on 'Cards'

  2. Select the card you want to add to your Apple/Google Wallet

  3. Click on Add to Apple Wallet or Add to Google Pay and follow the instructions

☝️ Good to know: you can add all your physical, virtual or Instant Qonto cards to your Apple/Google Wallet. You just need to follow the different steps depending on the chosen device.
