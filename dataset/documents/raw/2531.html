An AI-powered tool that automatically collects supplier invoices by scanning your transactions to identify suppliers, then retrieving invoices from their portals and matching them to transactions in Qonto.

This feature is currently in Beta phase.

 

Which plans and roles have access to this feature?

Available for all markets and for all plans. 

Only Owners and Admins roles can use the feature.

 

How does the invoice collection process work?

  1. Launch the integration and Qonto Operator scans your transactions to identify suppliers
  2. Log in to your supplier portals once
  3. Our AI agent automatically finds and downloads invoices
  4. Invoices are imported to Qonto and matched to transactions

 

Is my data secure when using this feature?

Yes. Qonto securely shares only recent transaction data with our partner Twin.so to identify suppliers.

Transaction data is not stored long-term outside of Qonto and is only used for supplier identification.

 

What are the key benefits?

  • Save time by importing invoices from suppliers in just a few clicks
  • Automatic invoice-to-transaction matching
  • Centralized invoice management
  • Faster bookkeeping and reporting

 

What is the Beta phase?

The Beta phase is the initial testing period where the AI Agent is being rolled out to a limited number of customers to ensure quality to gather feedback before its full release.
Customers in the Beta program get early access to this new feature on Qonto for testing purposes.
