Instant SEPA transfers

 

You can create instant SEPA transfers of up to €100,000. If your transfer is over €100,000 or your beneficiary cannot be reached via instant SEPA transfers, it will be carried out like a traditional transfer.

The owner, will also be able to set customizable limits for every instant transfer for himself or any other member who can make transfers.

You will be able to check those limits directly on your profile.

 

Standards SEPA transfers

 

You can create SEPA transfers without any amount limits to your beneficiary. If your transfer is greater than €30,000, you will be required to provide proof of payment as an attachment.

 

💡 To save time in your accounting, we advise you to add proof of payment for each transfer emitted.
