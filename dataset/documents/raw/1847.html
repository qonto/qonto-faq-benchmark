Streamline your supplier invoice management with Qonto and save valuable time: import all your supplier invoices with just two taps!

When you share photos and documents through WhatsApp, we automatically forward them to the Supplier invoices section in your Qonto account. From there, the magic happens 🪄—we extract data from the shared documents and automatically match this information with your payments.

You'll save significant time on collecting and matching documents!

This service is free and optional, available across all Qonto plans.

 

How can I set up the WhatsApp integration?

To activate the WhatsApp integration, use this link or refer to the email received from Qonto, in which you will also find a QR code.

  1. Scan the QR code with the mobile device associated with your Qonto account to open the WhatsApp conversation, or click on the link.
  2. Activate the integration by responding in the conversation.
  3. The integration becomes operational as soon as you complete this activation step 🎉

☝️ Important: The integration is initially set up for one company on Qonto. If you own multiple companies or have access to multiple accounts, make sure you have connected the correct company before sharing any documents.

 

Can I use this Integration to collect invoices for non-Qonto related payments?

If you're an account aggregation user with other bank accounts centralized in Qonto, this invoice collection feature also works for your aggregated payments. Whenever you make payments with any cards, send us the receipt, and we’ll match it with your payment on Qonto.

 

When are my invoices imported into Qonto?

We use emojis to update you on the status of documents sent via WhatsApp:

  • 🔎 = We are processing your document. This typically takes between 1-10 seconds.
  • ✅ = Your document is uploaded to the Qonto supplier invoice section. We perform a few magical operations to match this document to a transaction. If you don’t see the receipt attached to your payment, check the supplier invoice section.
  • ❌ = Oops! Something went wrong. Please try resending this document.
  • no emoji = Oops! The document wasn’t processed. Please resend it to Qonto.

 

What types of documents and files are accepted in the WhatsApp conversation?

Supported formats for documents and files sent to Qonto via WhatsApp include: HEIC, HEIF, JPEG, JPG, PNG, and PDF.

☝️ We do not support GIF, TXT, ZIP, and other unlisted formats.

Additionally, the maximum supported file size is 15MB.

Please note that if you send anything other than documents and files, we will automatically inform you that this channel is not intended for customer support inquiries. It is solely for sending your documents and files, or for changing the default organization when you have multiple Qonto accounts linked to your phone number.

 

What if I want to change the default organization?

If you have multiple bank accounts at Qonto under different names, this service groups all these companies based on your phone number.

You simply need to write to Qonto in the conversation, and we will allow you to select another Qonto account among those associated with your phone number.

☝️ If a company is missing from the list, it may be because you have not linked your phone number to that specific company.

Please add yourself as a user and link your phone number; the update will take place the next day on WhatsApp.

 

What if I want to opt-out?

To opt out of Qonto's WhatsApp notifications at any time, follow the instructions in our welcome message. Please note that opting out is not final; you can re-opt-in through the channel if you wish.

 

Can I share this conversation with other people from my team?

It is not possible to share this conversation with a third party.

However, team members who have user access to your Qonto account will automatically have access to the service.

Updates are processed every morning at 9:00AM.
