For legal and security reasons, it is important to always keep your personal and professional information up to date.

👆 Good to know: You can update all this information only via computer.

Follow the steps down below depending on your specific case:

👤 Updating personal information

  1. Log in to your web app 🖥️ and go to Settings (in the bottom left corner) > Organization details > Company profile.

  2. Click the Pencil button on your personal profile to update your information as shown in the following image:

  3. Remember to add a document justifying the modification of the information in the Submit an up-to-date proof of identity section.
    💡 As a reminder, here are the identity documents accepted by Qonto.

  4. Click on Save.

🏢 Updating my business information

❗️This update is only possible for companies. In order to change the address of your association, please contact Support at support@qonto.com to submit a modification request.

  1. You will need to add in the File an up-to-date registration document section a document justifying the modification of the information with a KBIS, INSEE's "Avis de situation", INPI's Document de synthèse définitive, or INPI's extrait d'immatriculation dating from less than 3 months.

  2. You can independently upload an image for your company logo or update your billing email address if you wish.

  3. Click on Save.

👥 Update of company leaders and shareholders.

If you are the corporate officer and legal representative of the company but it is not indicated, please correct the information:

❗️This is just an information update, but you won't be able to make a management change here.

  1. Select "yes":

  2. Submit an up-to-date registration document

  3. Fill in your tax information

  4. Save

  5. Confirm that your business information is up-to-date

✅ Our teams will then check and validate the new information.

❗️Only the account holder and members with an administrator role have the possibility to modify their information. If you do not have this role, we invite you to contact us.
