Qonto accepts the opening of a professional account for your works council under certain conditions.

💡 Only the following members can open an account on behalf of the CSE : 

  • The President
  • A committee member responsible for managing the account (as indicated in the minutes of the general assembly)

To ensure the smoothest and fastest account opening process with minimal interaction with customer service, we invite you to prepare for your registration and having the following documents ready:

  • The Kbis extract of the parent company, issued within the last 3 months, 
  • The extract from the minutes of the election of the committee members, including the President, Secretary, and Treasurer,
  • A copy of the minutes appointing the committee member(s) responsible for account management,
  • The ID of the committee member(s) (from the list of accepted IDs)

These documents will be requested by e-mail following your registration and contract signature.
