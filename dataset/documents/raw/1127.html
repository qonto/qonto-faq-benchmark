Main account

When you open a Qonto current account, your account name is Main account by default.

Want to rename it? 

You can personalize the account label to help you better identify your accounts (for example, by function, department, or with emojis):

  1. Go to the Business account 👉 Accounts section of your Qonto web app

  2. In your account's card, click on the three dots at the top right of the box

  3. Then, click on Rename and enter new account label (you can even add emojis)

Important: The official company name (as registered in the commercial register) is used by default for your Qonto account and appears on your legal documents. This name cannot be changed in the app.

The label you edit in the app is only for your own organization/visualization of your accounts and does not affect your official company name.

Multiple current accounts

If you have a Smart, Premium, Essential, Business or Enterprise plan, you can create multiple current accounts. You will be able to name it when you create it.

The label you choose will appear on your bank details and on your statements for easier identification, but your official company name remains unchanged and will still appear on legal documents. To know how to create multiple current accounts, you can read our article What are multiple accounts?
