In order to pay F24 through Qonto, the account Owner or Admin must activate the feature: it only takes a few minutes!

Once the feature has been activated, you can pay for all your F24 without limits and at no additional cost 🤩

 

How?

💻 From web

  1. Log in to your Qonto app on your computer, click on Taxes and Fees and then click on F24:

    • Qonto automatically fills in the Taxpayer section, so the first time you make an F24 payment, make sure that the information entered in this section is correct. These information will also be stored for future payments.

      If necessary, you can of course change the data for any payment and you will find the same pre-entered information when you make the next one.

    • Click on the sections for the F24 form relevant to your payment and enter the missing data and information: Authorities Tax, INPS, Regions, IMU, Other local taxes and Other social security and insurance bodies.

  2. Click on Continue, give your payment a name and then click on Continue again;

  3. If you have one or multiple accounts, choose the account you want to make the payment with;

  4. Verify that all the information entered are correct, click on Confirm and then on Close.

📱 From mobile

  1. Log in to your Qonto app on your mobile, click on Menu and then click on F24 
  2. Click on the + at the top right of the page and complete the sections with the data as above 👆

 

☝️ Good to know: before final confirmation, Qonto carries out the main checks on the model and automatically reports any compilation errors, so you can correct them before confirming the payment.

 

How long does it take to pay for an F24?

Filling an F24 takes only a few minutes! Once started, remember to finish it and do not interrupt it in order to avoid to repeat the process from the beginning.

From the time your F24 is filled out, the payment will be made immediately, but the amount will be charged between 11:30p.m. and the morning of the next day.

⚠️ Funds are withdrawn from the account in the night between the payment due date and the next day. Don't worry, even if the amount is withdrawn after midnight, the valid date for the Agenzia delle Entrate is the date selected at the time of payment.

☝️ For the payment to go through correctly, you must have sufficient funds in your account to cover the amount of the F24.

Please note:

  • You can schedule the exact date on which to pay your F24 up to 60 days before payment.

 

Who can fill out my F24?

The F24 form on the Qonto app can only be filled by the account Owner or Admin or by anyone with access to the account as an Accountant.
