Connecting your online accountant to your Qonto account simplifies your accounting by providing easy access to financial data and ensuring a smooth management process. This synchronization reduces the risk of errors.

How to connect my online accountant?

To connect your online accountant to your Qonto account, follow these steps:

  1. Get your API key:
    • Log in to your Qonto account and go to the integrations section.
  2. Access your online accountant’s platform:
    • Log in to your account on your online accountant’s website.
  3. Enter the API key in the account synchronization section:
    • In the section dedicated to synchronizing professional accounts on your online accountant’s platform, enter your API key in the designated field.

If you have any questions or face difficulties, contact your online accountant directly. They can assist you with the connection process.

What to do if my online accountant is not listed in the integrations?

If your online accountant is not listed among the available integrations, you can request it by sending an email to: accounting.partners@qonto.com. We will review the possibility of adding this integration to our list.

How to connect Qonto with ComptaPlace?

To sync your Qonto account with ComptaPlace, follow the steps below:

  1. Click here
  2. Create your LegalPlace Pro account by clicking on Start for free.

You will then be redirected to the creation of your LegalPlace Pro account, the accounting management platform. To complete this step, you will need to provide:

  • Your personal information (first name, last name, email address)
  • Your company information (SIREN, tax regime, etc.)
  1. Sync your Qonto account

After creating your account, simply click on Qonto on the synchronization screen. A window will open where you can select your Qonto account to finalize the connection.
